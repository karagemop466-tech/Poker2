F-01 · No strict single-entry candidate confirmed; some Wynn examples explicitly permit re-entry

Why flagged: The Venetian schedule/structures provide event times, buy-ins, guarantees, chips and levels, but the structure sheets checked do not expressly prohibit re-entry, rebuy or add-on. Wynn's $600/$800 featured NLH events allow unlimited re-entry per flight, and its WPT Prime event allows one re-entry per flight; those rows are marked “Re-entry allowed.”

Impact: No event can yet be verified as satisfying “single entry only, no spending advantage.” Venetian entry caps remain unverified; the highlighted Wynn re-entry events are explicitly excluded into the separate section.

Needed: Event-by-event official confirmation in writing, including multiple flights, same-player entry maximum, re-entry, add-on/rebuy, and registration after busting.

F-03 · Venetian poker-rate page has a rate/fee presentation ambiguity

Why flagged: The official 2026 poker-rate page says rates start at $119/$209, then states “There is a $55 resort fee per night.” The separate official fee page says “Rates include a daily Resort Fee of $55 plus tax.” The precise meaning for the quoted poker offer should be confirmed at booking.

Impact: The $197.28 / $299.32 amounts on the Hotels page are arithmetic illustrations assuming the resort fee is additional and both components are taxed; they are not verified booking totals. The only safe exact-price field for the planned trip remains blank.

Needed: Ask for the room, resort fee, taxes and full stay total in writing with exact dates and the EPOKR25 offer.

F-04 · Venetian poker hotel rate requires substantial play

Confirmed condition: The 2026 poker-rate page requires six hours of active live cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in for each night stayed. If the requirement is missed, the nightly price adjusts to the prevailing rate. This condition changes the meaningful hotel cost and may create a spending incentive.

Impact: The starting rate cannot be treated as a guaranteed cheap room for the full vacation. Confirm whether the desired itinerary naturally meets the condition and whether the rate is available on the actual dates.

F-05 · Wynn's event terms vary; several high-guarantee entries allow re-entry

Confirmed: The official WPT structures allow unlimited re-entry per starting flight for the $600 NLH $400K and $800 NLH $1M events, and one entry plus one re-entry per starting flight for WPT Prime. Those three events are marked and separated. Wynn general terms require checking event-specific structures and reserve the right to revise, suspend or cancel events; not every other event's entry rule was confirmed.

Impact: Big posted guarantees are not proof of single entry, current availability, or a guaranteed field size. These re-entry terms disqualify the noted events from the strict list.
