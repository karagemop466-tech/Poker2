How flags are assigned

high
The data cannot be used as-is for a decision. Either a required value is missing entirely (F-03, no dated room rate) or two official sources contradict each other on a number that matters (F-05, Bellagio's Saturday event).
medium
The value is usable but carries a stated caveat the reader must know: a stale banner (F-07), a starting rate that is conditional (F-04), a header that names the wrong month (F-06).
low
Cosmetic or documentary: an event number printed inconsistently (F-12), a third-party routing source used for walking distances (F-11).

A flag is never a reason to leave a cell empty without saying why. Each table row that depends on a flagged value links to this page from its caption or its venue page.

The three that would change the plan most

F-03 / Q-09 — no dated hotel rate exists. Every booking flow read returned placeholders. The trip cannot be costed. This is the single largest gap in the dataset.

F-05 — Bellagio's Saturday $1,100 event. The structure PDF promises a $50,000 guarantee but allocates only $600-per-player to the prize pool, which cannot fund a $50,000 guarantee at any plausible field size. One of the two printed numbers is wrong; the event is left out of the ranking rather than guessed at.

F-01 — zero confirmed single-entry events. Not a defect in the research but a finding: every property that publishes an entry rule publishes a way to pay twice. The single-entry short-list is empty because the market is, not because the search stopped early.
