Caesars Palace

Official guide: four NLH events daily (10 AM, 2 PM, 6 PM, 9 PM), $100 buy-in, 20,000 chips, $1,000 GTD, 20-minute blinds, $50 add-on for 15,000 chips at the first break.

Horseshoe Las Vegas (Hall of Fame Poker Room)

Official guide: six NLH events daily (9 AM, 11 AM, 2 PM, 5 PM, 8 PM, 11 PM) — “a mixture of buy-ins, levels and guarantees”; values not published there.

MGM Grand

Official page: daily NLH tournaments at 11:00 AM, 7:00 PM and 11:00 PM. Buy-ins live on the official weekly-schedule PDF (not re-read this session).

Official page: daily at 10 AM / 5 PM / 2 AM, 15,000 chips, 15-minute levels, unlimited re-entry until level 5 and up to two $20 add-ons. Buy-in not printed on the page.
