The 30-minute walking rule

The constraint was that a hotel must be within a 30-minute walk of a tournament. Here is exactly how far that constraint can be enforced from official sources today.

Step in the ruleWhat official sources give us Status
Is the poker room in the hotel?Published for all six venues, with the room's floor or wing (column 2 above). The Venetian and Palazzo towers share one complex with the poker room; Wynn's poker room sits inside Encore.published
How long is the walk?Nothing. No retrieved official page states a walking duration, distance or route between a Strip hotel and a poker room.not published
Apply the 30-minute capCannot be applied mechanically without the previous step. Applying it would mean inventing the number the cap is applied to.blocked
Practical consequenceEvery property on this page is either in the same building as a poker room or directly connected to one, so none is plausibly beyond a 30-minute indoor walk — but plausibly is the site owner's inference, not a verified fact, and it is therefore not used to rank anything.unverified

To close this: time one walk per hotel–room pair, record the date, the route and whether it was indoors, and the cap becomes checkable. That is Q-05 on the Questions page and F-03 on Flagged.
