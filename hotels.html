Conditional poker-player rate — not an automatic recommendation

The Venetian's official poker-player rate page says rates start at $119 for a Luxury King Suite at The Venetian or $209 at The Palazzo, plus a $55 resort fee per night. This is a starting rate, not a quote or guaranteed current price; subject to occupancy/availability and qualifying play. The published conditions include six hours of active live cash play or buying into a tournament of $300 or higher for each night stayed, with play requirement deadlines; if unmet, the nightly rate adjusts to prevailing rate. It also describes allowable upgrades/occupancy charges.

Keep this separate: a rate whose eligibility requires tournament or cash-game spending/time can change the trip's economics, and is not counted as a low-cost hotel option without confirming that the itinerary naturally meets the condition and verifying today's final quote.

How to calculate the true nightly cost

  1. Obtain the official property quote for exact arrival and departure dates, room type, and guest count.
  2. Record the full lodging total, including room rate, mandatory resort fee, and lodging taxes; divide by nights to compare true per-night cost.
  3. Keep refundable/nonrefundable terms, parking, deposits, extra-guest charges, and any conditional player-rate requirement as separate decision factors.
  4. Confirm accessible walking route and time to the actual poker room entrance. Do not infer it from straight-line distance or map pin distance.

The Venetian terms publish 13.38% Clark County room tax and explain it is excluded from daily rates. Do not apply that rate blindly to every fee or property; obtain the booking checkout total. Official terms.
