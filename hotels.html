The one number nobody publishes

True nightly cost = room rate + mandatory resort fee + tax on both. Three of those four inputs are verified for five properties. The room rate is not, and it is the largest input.

Why: The Venetian's booking calendar and the Caesars booking flow both render $XXX price placeholders to a client that is not a browser, and Wynn's room pages return a "this page has checked out" stub. The published figures that do exist — $119 and $209 on the Venetian poker-rate page — are starting rates, conditional on six hours of live cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in per night, offered by phone only on code EPOKR25, and capped at 150 days ahead. They are not quotes.

Ranking logic, and why no hotel is ranked here

The brief ranks cost and quality above distance, with a hard 30-minute walk gate. That ranking needs a cost, and the only cost inputs available are fees. Ranking on fees alone would put MGM Grand first at $50/night — and MGM Grand fails the distance gate to the Venetian at 39 minutes. Ranking on the published starting rate alone would put the Venetian first at $119/night — and that rate is conditional on playing six hours every single night.

So this page states the constraints and refuses the ranking until a real rate exists. That is deliberate: a ranking built on a starting rate would look authoritative and be wrong.
