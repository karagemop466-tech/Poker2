Venetian poker-rate math (illustrative floor)

For Venetian: official starting range is $119 (Venetian) / $209 (Palazzo) plus $55 resort fee per night. Official hotel terms identify Clark County room tax as 13.38%. Applying 13.38% to both components yields approximately $197.28 / $299.32. The resort’s page says the rate can be higher, is subject to occupancy/availability/blackouts, and requires either six hours of active live cash play or buy-in to a $300+ tournament for each night. If the requirement is missed, the rate adjusts to the prevailing rate. Exact fee treatment and current booking quote must be confirmed at reservation.

Wynn fee

Wynn's official Resort Information page lists a $55 daily fee plus tax. No room price for the planned dates has been verified. Wynn's page lists bundled resort amenities, but those amenities are not deducted from the cash cost.

Hotel recommendation, with limits: If the final schedule is mostly Venetian events, first check The Venetian’s or Palazzo’s direct dates because the tournament and lodging are on the same resort property. Treat the poker rate as conditional rather than cheap lodging; it may encourage extra live play. If the schedule centers on Wynn's WPT festival, compare direct Wynn/Encore rates against Venetian after exact fees and the hotel route are confirmed. These are venue-fit suggestions, not a verified quality/price ranking.
