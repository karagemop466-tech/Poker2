FD-03Silence on a structure sheet is not a single-entry ruleNone of the 12 Venetian structure sheets read in this pass prints an entry limit for a one-day event. Each prints its fee split, level time, late-registration close and payout percentage, and none prints 'no re-entry', 'no rebuy' or 'no add-on'. Six sheets print a flight restriction instead: 'Any player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a subsequent flight'.A row labelled 'single entry' on that basis would be an inference. The honest label is 'sheet silent', and the decision to treat silence as a freezeout has to be made explicitly, in writing, by the player or by the venue.sheets 1, 5, 7, 11, 15, 16, 18, 34 read in full; none prints an entry-limit clause.venetianlasvegas.comEvery row carries single_entry = not_stated / explicit_no / explicit_yes, and the site never upgrades silence.
