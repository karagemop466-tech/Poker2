Rate log

A rate here is a dated observation with a source, not a standing value. The log keeps the rows that failed as well as the rows that succeeded, so the next capture has something to be compared against.

A complete row needs: arrival_date; departure_date; nights; occupancy; room_type; rate_plan; nightly_rate; resort_fee; tax; parking; total_at_checkout; cancellation_deadline; source_url; captured_at.

Why there is no dated total. Every official booking engine found (venetianlasvegas.com/book, wynnlasvegas.com/book, the MGM property sites, caesars.com) renders date-specific prices only inside JavaScript. A static site cannot read them, and no request from this workspace returned a price. No dated total is therefore claimed. This is the single largest gap on the site.
