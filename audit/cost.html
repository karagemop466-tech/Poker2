Trip cost

The formula is entries × buy-in + nights × true hotel cost. The first half is computed exactly from published numbers. The second half cannot be, because no dated room rate exists, so the calculator returns the part it can prove and names the part it cannot. Pick the entries on the left; the hotel line will say “unknown” rather than invent a number.

What each line is

entries × buy-inA published buy-in from the official schedule page. Exact.
fee withheldPrinted on the structure sheet as “$X of the $Y Buy-In is withheld for house fees”. It is already inside the buy-in and is never added on top.
nights × resort feeThe property's own published nightly fee, where one was read.
tax13.38% Clark County transient lodging tax, applied to rate and fee. That the fee is taxed comes from the properties' wording (“plus applicable tax”); it is not a printed arithmetic. See IQ-04.
parkingVenetian and Palazzo $20/day for registered guests; free self-parking is claimed for South Point by an earlier session and not re-read; nothing published for the rest.
room rateMissing. No dated quote was obtainable. See FD-05.
