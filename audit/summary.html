What is solid

  • 94 schedule rows transcribed from the official page.
  • 12 structure sheets read in full; every fee split sums exactly to the buy-in (0 mismatches).
  • Room tax 13.38% corroborated by a county document and by the property's own terms.
  • Walking times measured for all eleven hotel candidates, with re-checkable URLs.
  • Late-registration closes and payout percentage for 48 rows — values the earlier dashboards left blank.

What is not

  • No confirmed single-entry event. Twelve sheets, no entry-limit clause.
  • No dated room rate. The hotel side of the trip cost has no verified input.
  • 46 rows have no fee or structure, because 25 sheets were not read.
  • Four hotels publish no reachable resort fee.
  • No automatic source check in this repository has ever completed a scheduled run (FD-01).

If you had to decide today

  1. Pick a 4-night window between 2026-11-19 and 2026-12-22: every day in that range has at least one one-day event.
  2. Stay at The Venetian or The Palazzo: 0 and 7 minutes on foot, the only properties whose poker-suite rate is published, and the only ones where a $300+ buy-in also satisfies that rate's qualifying play.
  3. Make a $200 RaceDay Stack (10:10 AM, $35 fee) or a $300/$600 one-day (11:10 AM, $58/$90 fee) the primary each day, and treat a 5:10 PM event as a fallback only if you bust before its late-registration close.
  4. Do not treat any of them as single-entry until the poker room confirms it in writing (IQ-03).
  5. Get an itemised checkout total for the dates before booking, and record it in the rate log.
