A trip planner built only from documents a venue or a county publishes. Every row links to the page or PDF it came from, carries the date it was checked, and leaves the cell blank when the document does not print the value. Three things on this site are uncomfortable and are stated anyway: no event is confirmed single-entry, no dated hotel rate exists, and an 8 AM start is not on any schedule.

How to read the labels

sheet silent
the structure sheet was read in full and prints no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or entry-limit clause. That is silence, not a published freezeout rule.
explicit: no 2nd entry
the sheet prints a rule that stops a player entering twice. On every read sheet this is the flight rule “any player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a subsequent flight”.
explicit: re-entry allowed
the sheet prints a mechanism for a second entry. Those rows are kept out of the one-entry section.
includes bounties
the guarantee is defined on the sheet as prize pool plus total bounties, so its ratio is not comparable with a pure prize-pool guarantee.
not read
the sheet is linked from the official page but was not opened in this pass. Fee, stack, level time and late-registration close are blank.

Built from official venue pages and structure-sheet PDFs, a county tax document and a published walking-routing service. Blank means not confirmed, never estimated. Data: 2026-11-17. Root dashboard · /plan/ · Repository · COORDINATION.md
