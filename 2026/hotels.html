The poker suite rate, verbatim

We offer suite rates for poker players who are interested in staying in either The Venetian or The Palazzo. These rates will start as low as $119 and $209 and can be higher. Future rates are only provided out to a maximum of 150 days from the current date. Rates are subject to qualifying poker play and availability based on hotel occupancy. Blackout dates may apply. … There is a $55 resort fee per night stayed.
During your stay, you will be required to play six hours of active live cash gameplay or buy in to a $300 or higher tournament for each night stayed. … These requirements must be met prior to 2 a.m. the last night stayed. For any nights you fail to complete these requirements, your nightly suite rate will be adjusted to the prevailing rate, and you will not receive the poker suite rate discount.
In order to qualify for these rates, the first step is to book your suite through The Venetian Resort Las Vegas by calling 866.255.8936 and mentioning code EPOKR25.

Source: www.venetianlasvegas.com, retrieved 2026-11-19. The poker rate cannot be combined with any other promotional rate, and the page does not state a cancellation deadline.
