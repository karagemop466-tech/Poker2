What the official document says, in its own words

Fridays — NLH $600 buy-in

Re-entry. “3. Players may buy-in and re-enter through the end of level 8 (Including Break). Tournament Surrender is allowed on Level 8 break.”
Split. “5. The $600 Buy-In is allocated as follows: $510 to Prize Pool; $50 House Fee; $40 Voluntary Staff Gratuity.”
Gratuity buys chips. “7. The voluntary staff gratuity will grant players an extra 15,000 chips. This optional gratuity is paid to staff and will not be kept by the house.”
Starting stack. “1. Players will start with 30,000 tournament chips, including the optional Staff Gratuity. (15,000 + 15,000)”
official structure sheet (PDF)
  • No guarantee is printed on this sheet.
  • No end time is printed.

Saturdays — NLH (sheet headline: $1,100 buy-in)

Combined prize pool. “8. Bellagio guarantees that the total Combined Prize Pool will be at least $50,000.”
Split (prints $600). “7. Of the $600 Buy-in, $510 goes to the Prize Pool, $50 is Admin Fees and $40 is optional staff gratuity.”
Gratuity buys chips. “9. … There is a voluntary staff gratuity of $40, at registration only. The voluntary staff gratuity will grant players an extra 20,000 chips.”
Registration close. “3. Registration will be open until the start of Level 10. (Approximately 5:15pm for Flights 1A, 1B, 4:15pm for 1C and 9:30pm for Flight D).”
Fixed date in the sheet. “4. Combined Final Day 2 (9/13/26) will begin at 12pm at Bellagio.”
official structure sheet (PDF)
  • The sheet's headline reads “NLH $1100 BUY-IN” while rule 7 splits a $600 buy-in. The two figures cannot both be right; the buy-in is left blank and flagged rather than picked from one of the two.
  • The only final-day date printed is 9/13/26, which is already past on 2026-11-17, so this sheet may be a stale series document rather than the current weekly event.
  • No end time is printed.

Sundays — $400 H.O.R.S.E. Tournament

Re-entry. “5. Players may buy-in and re-enter through the end of level 8 (Including Break). Tournament Surrender is allowed on Level 8 break.”
Split. “1. The $400 Buy-In is allocated as follows: $340 to Prize Pool; $35 House Fee; $25 Voluntary Staff Gratuity.”
Gratuity buys chips. “2. … A voluntarty [sic] Staff gratuity of $25, at registration only, will grant players an additional 15,000 in starting chips.”
Payouts. “7. Payout structure based on number of entries and will be approximately 12.5% of field.”
official structure sheet (PDF)
  • No guarantee is printed on this sheet.
  • No end time is printed.
