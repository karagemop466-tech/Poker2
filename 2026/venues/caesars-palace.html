What the official document does not print

  • No end time is printed for any of the four events, so no sequence can be promised.
  • No single-entry, freezeout or no-re-entry clause is printed; rule 3 expressly allows re-entry.
  • The guarantee is printed as a flat $1,000 for all four events with no field-size condition.
  • The flyer is titled for 2026 but prints no series dates, so it is read as a standing daily schedule rather than a dated calendar.

What the official document says, in its own words

Add-on. “All Tournaments feature a $50 add-on for 15,000 tournament chips that can only be taken at the first break. Registration will close at the start of level seven. All tournaments will have a 10-minute break every six levels.”
Re-entry. “3. Participants may only register themselves, but may re-enter, by paying the entry fee, upon elimination within the first break of play.”
Registration. “1. Sign-ups start one hour before the scheduled start of the event. 2. Registration is open until the start of level seven.”
Buy-in split ($100). “6. Of the $100 buy in, $70 goes to the prize pool and $30 is held by Caesars Palace as a maintenance fee.”
Buy-in split ($160). “7. Of the $160 buy in, $115 goes to the prize pool and $45 is held by Caesars Palace as a maintenance fee.”
Add-on split. “8. Of the $50 add-on, $45 goes to the prize pool and $5 is held by Caesars Palace as a maintenance fee.”
Minimum field. “26. Must have at least 6 players to start a tournament. If a tournament does not have at least 6 players at the scheduled start time the tournament will be canceled, and refunds will be issued”
Chops. “27. Prize pool may not be chopped until the end of registration.”
