How to improve decision-making on this site

  1. Close the single-entry gap first. Everything else is refined guesswork until the room states its re-entry policy in writing. One email. S-01
  2. Replace modelled walk times with measured ones. The 30-minute rule is the user's own priority. Google Maps and OSRM both estimate a route; neither measures time from the room door to the table. S-04
  3. Turn the monitor from page hashes into value changes. A hash change tells you a page moved. Tracking the specific sentence each row was transcribed from tells you the buy-in changed. S-07
  4. Pick one quality authority. Cost and quality outrank distance in the brief, but quality has no official source, so it is currently a blank column. S-06
  5. Consolidate. Three scheduled monitor jobs and four overlapping dashboards are still in the repository. S-09, S-10

Data policy. Every value on this site is transcribed from an official venue document that was read and is linked on every row. A value the organiser does not publish is left empty and listed on Flagged for review. Routing and cost estimates are labeled separately. Core structure-sheet audit: 2026-11-17.
