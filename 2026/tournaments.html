How to read the ranking. Guarantee ÷ entry fee is shown only where both numbers are printed on the official documents, and it is a descriptive ratio, not a forecast: a guarantee is a floor the room commits to, not a predicted pool. Bounty and PKO events are in their own group because their published guarantee includes bounty money, so the cash prize pool is smaller than the headline figure. Multi-flight events are separate because entering a second flight is a second buy-in. Satellites pay in seats, not cash.

Data policy. Every value on this site is transcribed from an official venue document that was read and is linked on every row. A value the organiser does not publish is left empty and listed on Flagged for review. Routing and cost estimates are labeled separately. Core structure-sheet audit: 2026-11-17.

This is a research tool, not a booking service. Re-confirm every official page before you pay anything non-refundable. This /2026/ surface is generated by scripts/build2026.py; the site root hosts the older multi-venue dashboard, and the two do not share a generator.
