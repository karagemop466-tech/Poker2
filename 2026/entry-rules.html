Entry rules, quoted

Single-entry, re-entry, add-on and fee values are stored as separate fields for every event, each with the sentence it was taken from and the date it was read. Nothing here is a summary of what a sheet probably means. Where a sheet says nothing, that is recorded as silence — and silence is not a prohibition.

The one thing to understand. All 37 official structure sheets were read on 2026-11-17. Not one contains a one-entry-only rule. Where a restriction is absent, it stays unverified even when the fee, starting stack and schedule are confirmed. Flag F-01.
