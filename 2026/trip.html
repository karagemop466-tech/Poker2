Trip cost estimator

Estimated cost of a trip = entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost per night. Both halves use only figures printed on official documents: entry fees from the structure sheets, and hotel cost from the resort fee, tax and rate you enter below.

Entries

Pick the events you plan to enter. Each entry is one buy-in at the official total entry fee. Multi-flight events cost one buy-in per flight entered — if you plan to fire two flights, add the event twice.

Data policy. Every value on this site is transcribed from an official venue document that was read and is linked on every row.
