Day plan

One primary event per day, plus a conditional fallback where one exists. The plan never claims you can play two events in sequence, because no official document publishes a finish time. What is published is each event's start time and its registration close — and the registration close is the real deadline: it is the latest moment you can still take your seat in the later event.

Method. For each date, the earliest-starting genuine entry is the primary. Every later entry on the same date is a fallback, conditional on your being out of the primary before the fallback's registration closes. Where a fallback's registration closes before the primary even starts, the pair is flagged cannot be sequenced. Twelve of the rows in the official schedule are Day 2, Day 3 or streamed final tables; they are listed as continuations, not entries, because you cannot buy into them.
