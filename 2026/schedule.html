Day plan

No strict single-entry itinerary is confirmed. The research view shows one lead per day and other starts separately. A conditional fallback is not a booking recommendation. The view never claims you can play two events in sequence, because no official document publishes a finish time. What is published is each event's start time and its registration close — and the registration close is the real deadline: it is the latest moment you can still take your seat in the later event.

Provisional research plan, not an eligible freezeout itinerary. No event currently meets the confirmed-only policy. The primary below is a research option, not a booking recommendation. How it is chosen: the day's research option among unrestricted rules-silent one-day candidates. If no such candidate exists, there is no primary. Bounty, multi-flight, satellite, team and restricted events remain separate alternatives rather than planned buy-ins. Earlier start breaks a tie, then the larger guarantee. Age- or gender-restricted events are never promoted, but they are listed under Also running so an eligible player still sees them. Other entries are not planned buy-ins. A different unrestricted candidate can only be a fallback if you are out before its registration closes and both entry rules have been confirmed; where a fallback's registration closes before the primary starts, the pair is flagged cannot be sequenced. Twelve rows in the official schedule are Day 2, Day 3 or streamed final tables; they are listed as continuations, because you cannot buy into them.
