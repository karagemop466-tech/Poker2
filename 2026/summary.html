What was verified

  • All 37 official Venetian structure sheets were read individually on 2026-11-17, covering all 64 event numbers in the series. Each was transcribed field by field: buy-in split, late-registration close, starting stack, level length, payout share, bounty mechanic, and whether any re-entry, rebuy or add-on clause appears.
  • The official schedule was read twice, from the HTML event page and from the schedule PDF, which prints registration-close times, chip counts and level lengths the HTML page does not. The two agree on every row.
  • The hotel numbers come from the official resort-fee page and the official poker suite-rate page, both read on 2026-11-17.
  • Seven other poker rooms were checked. None publishes enough to rank even one event.

What could not be verified

  • Any single-entry guarantee. Not one official document says an event is one entry only. The strongest available statement is the absence of a re-entry clause. F-01
  • Any finish time. No event has a published end time, so no two events can be promised as a sequence. F-04
  • Any dated hotel quote. Official booking engines render prices with JavaScript. F-05
  • Any official quality rating. No hotel page publishes a star or diamond rating. S-06
  • Any measured walking time. The matrix is a routing estimate with a Google Maps link on every row. F-08

The decision this all rests on

You asked for single-entry tournaments only, and for anything where money buys an advantage to be kept in a separate section. The documents let us do the second half cleanly: multi-flight events, satellites, team events, bounty formats and the two Valuetown events that price a second buy-in are all in their own groups.

The first half cannot be completed from the documents alone. No official sheet states a one-entry rule. Sixteen events are one-day, single-starting-day, and carry no re-entry, rebuy or add-on clause at all — which is the strongest evidence available, and is still silence rather than a promise. One email to the poker room closes that gap. See S-01 and Q-01.
