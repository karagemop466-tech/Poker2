Flagged for review

Every value that could not be confirmed from an official source, and every irregularity found in an official document. Nothing here blocks the site — it is the list of things you should look at yourself, with the link to do it.

Two of these are defects in the official documents, not gaps in our reading. X-01: the official schedule PDF prints a sponsor banner where the date should be on twelve rows. X-02: the official series overview still calls an ended series its “Current Series”.

F-01Entry rulesNo Venetian structure sheet states a single-entry or freezeout rule
All 37 official structure sheets were read on 2026-11-17. Not one contains the words 'single entry', 'one entry', 'freezeout' or 'no re-entry'. Every one-day sheet is simply silent on re-entry, rebuy and add-on. Silence is not a prohibition, so no event is marked 'single-entry confirmed'.
The single-entry filter can only offer 'no re-entry/add-on clause printed', never 'confirmed'.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-02Entry rulesTwo events price a second buy-in on the official sheet
Events 18 and 58 ($200 Valuetown Bounty) print: 'PokerAtlas PRO players will receive a twenty ($20) rake discount on their initial $200 buy in only ... All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200.' Pricing a subsequent buy-in means more than one buy-in per player is contemplated.
Events 18 and 58 are moved out of the single-entry candidate group.www.venetianlasvegas.com
X-01Official document defectThe official schedule PDF has a garbled date column on 12 rows
In dse-nov-2026-schedule.pdf the DATE cell on 12 rows prints a sponsor banner ('@PokerAtlas PRO', '@PokerAtlas* TOUR') or a series logo ('LIPS CHAMPIONSHIP ...') instead of the date. Those rows are events 17, 18, 32, 33, 35, 36, 39, 40, 46, 47 (1A), 49 (1A and 1B) and 58.
Every affected date was recovered from the official HTML schedule page and is marked 'date from HTML' in the row detail. The PDF alone is not sufficient to place those events.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-03Prize poolsBounty guarantees include bounty money, so they are not like-for-like
Bounty and PKO sheets state the guarantee 'consists of the actual prize pool plus the total amount of bounties that are awarded'. A $8,000 bounty guarantee is therefore a smaller cash prize pool than a $8,000 non-bounty guarantee.
Bounty events are shown in a separate group and are never mixed into the like-for-like ranking.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-04ScheduleNo official finish time is published for any event
The schedule gives start time, registration close, chips and level length, but no end time. A finish time cannot be derived without a field size, which the organiser does not publish in advance.
Overlap flags are computed as 'same-day entries cannot both be played from the start'. The later event is offered as a conditional fallback whose deadline is its official registration close. Nothing is presented as a guaranteed finish.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-05HotelsNo dated room quote was captured
Official booking engines render rates with client-side JavaScript, so no dated checkout total, nightly average or rate timestamp could be read on 2026-11-17. The Venetian's own page also says future poker rates 'are only provided out to a maximum of 150 days from the current date' and must be booked by phone.
The true-cost calculator needs you to enter the nightly rate you are quoted; it is not prefilled.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-06HotelsCancellation deadlines are partial: two properties publish 72 hours, the rest do not
Partly resolved on 2026-11-17 by reading each property's own terms page first-hand. Bellagio and MGM Grand both publish a 72-hour window: the first night's deposit is fully refundable if Room Reservations is told at least 72 hours before arrival, and a late cancellation or no-show forfeits it. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe advertise 'FREE 48-Hour Cancellation*' with an asterisk whose condition is not printed on the page read. The Venetian and Palazzo publish no poker-rate deadline, but do publish that prepaid offers are non-cancellable and non-refundable unless the offer says otherwise, and that checking out early costs $100 plus tax. Wynn publishes deposit terms only (one night charged at booking) and no refund window.
For a Bellagio or MGM Grand stay you can plan a refundable hold until 72 hours out. For the Venetian poker rate, which is the rate this plan depends on, the deadline is still not established, so treat the deposit as committed.bellagio.mgmresorts.com
F-07TaxesRoom tax: the hotel's own terms are re-read, the county levy is not
13.38% comes from Clark County's own 'General Information Guide for Transient Lodging Establishments' and is corroborated by the Venetian's hotel terms. It was read on 2026-11-16 by an earlier session and was not re-read today.
Used as the calculator default. Re-check on the county page before you book.www.clarkcountynv.gov
F-08WalkingWalk times are routing estimates, not a Google or Apple Maps measurement
The walk matrix was computed from OpenStreetMap data with the OSRM foot profile (routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot) on 2026-11-16. A Google Maps walking-directions link is published on every row so you can confirm each pair yourself.
The 30-minute filter is applied to the routed estimate and is labelled as an estimate.www.openstreetmap.org
F-09Other venuesThe Wynn poker citation on this site pointed at a page that no longer exists
https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/casino/poker-room returns Wynn's 'IT SEEMS THIS PAGE HAS CHECKED OUT' not-found page, which is why the scheduled re-check logged HTTP 404 for the Wynn row on 2026-11-17. The room's live page is https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/casino/poker, read there first-hand on 2026-11-17, and every Wynn citation on this site now points at it. What that page does *not* publish: a buy-in for any daily event, any one-entry or re-entry limit for the daily slate, or a start time other than the 6 p.m. evening guarantee. The WPT structures PDF lines still come from the 2026-11-16 reading and stay labelled as such.
Until the room states an entry limit, no Wynn event can leave the 'not single-entry confirmed' group - the page was reachable, the missing values are genuinely unpublished, not unfetched.www.wynnlasvegas.com
F-10Other venuesOnly The Venetian publishes a dated schedule plus a per-event structure sheet
Every other Strip room checked either publishes no tournament schedule, no structure sheet, or both. Horseshoe publishes six daily start times with no buy-ins; Resorts World's poker page redirects to a dining page; Westgate publishes room hours only; MGM's weekly grid carries no year; Bellagio's Saturday sheet contradicts itself.
The ranked tables are Venetian DSE IV only. That is a coverage limit, not a preference.www.caesars.com
F-11HotelsA per-night rate is not the bill: occupancy and early-departure charges
The properties charge more than the nightly rate and resort fee in two ways a rate comparison misses. The Venetian and Palazzo add $50 per night for each additional guest 13 or older above double occupancy, and $100 plus tax for checking out before the scheduled departure date. Bellagio adds $50 plus tax per occupant per night above two. MGM Grand adds $40 plus tax per occupant per night above two. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe do not publish an occupancy charge on the page read.
As a pair, the published rates and the calculator are right. With a third adult in the room the nightly cost rises by $50 (Venetian) or $40 (MGM Grand) plus tax, which can exceed the difference between two hotels.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-12HotelsCaesars Palace and Horseshoe do not publish a resort fee on the page read
Both properties' official pages were read on 2026-11-17: the resort-hotel page, the casino page and the hotel page. None prints a resort-fee amount; only a 48-hour cancellation headline and 'valet and self-parking available' appear. Figures of $54.95 and $49.95 are widely repeated by aggregator sites and are deliberately not used here.
Those two properties cannot be costed on this site, so they cannot win a cost comparison. They stay in the walking tables because distance is published.www.caesars.com

F-14Other venuesTwo official Caesars documents print different daily buy-ins for the same room
Caesars' poker-tournaments page, re-read first-hand on 2026-11-17, prints '$100 buy-in ... $1,000 guaranteed prize pool' for the four daily Caesars Palace events. The property's own daily NLH flyer, read 2026-11-16, prints $160 for the 2 p.m. and 9 p.m. starts. Both are official; neither corrects the other. Nothing on this site picks a winner: the room is listed with both citations and is excluded from the ranking regardless, because the page also prints a $50 add-on.
Any comparison of a Caesars daily against a Venetian DSE event is unsafe on price alone. Ask the room, or treat the higher figure as the cost of the seat.www.caesars.com
F-15TaxesThe county tax guide's own title and its URL disagree by three years
The Clark County transient-lodging guide the room-tax figure is cited from is served at a slug ending 'july-2021.pdf' while the document's internal title reads 'Genl Info Guide for TLEs Sept 2018'. The rate itself was confirmed first-hand on 2026-11-17 from the licence register inside that document, which lists the Venetian / Palazzo at 13.38% under 'Transient Lodging Tax - Resort Hotel (PGC)', and the same 13.38% is printed in the Venetian's own hotel terms.
The 13.38% used by the calculators is corroborated by two documents, but the county copy is not fresh-dated. Re-check the current rate with the room's checkout itemisation before you treat a true-cost figure as final.www.clarkcountynv.gov
