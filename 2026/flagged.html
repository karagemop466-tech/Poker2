All 37 official structure sheets were read on 2026-11-17. Not one contains the words 'single entry', 'one entry', 'freezeout' or 'no re-entry'. Every one-day sheet is simply silent on re-entry, rebuy and add-on. Silence is not a prohibition, so no event is marked 'single-entry confirmed'.

Events 18 and 58 are moved out of the single-entry candidate group.www.venetianlasvegas.com

F-03Prize poolsBounty guarantees include bounty money, so they are not like-for-like
Bounty and PKO sheets state that the guarantee 'consists of the actual prize pool plus the total amount of bounties that are awarded'. An $8,000 bounty guarantee does not guarantee $8,000 in the non-bounty pool; the eventual pool is not known in advance.
Bounty events are shown in a separate group and are never mixed into the like-for-like ranking.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-04ScheduleNo official finish time is published for any event
The schedule gives start time, registration close, chips and level length, but no end time. A finish time cannot be derived without a field size, which the organiser does not publish in advance.
The day plan compares start order with the later registration deadline. A later event is conditional, not a guaranteed sequence; no unconfirmed event becomes a primary.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-05HotelsNo like-for-like checkout totals were confirmed
A Venetian December 1–4 lookup for one adult loaded an official booking shell without a price. A November 25–28 lookup for two adults also produced no itemized total. Earlier root Venetian quote transcriptions and a partial Wynn rate use different dates and cannot be compared with either example stay.
Do not rank hotels without comparable, timestamped checkout totals and offer-specific terms.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-06HotelsCancellation deadlines are partial: two properties publish 72 hours, the rest do not
Partly resolved on 2026-11-17 by reading each property's own terms page first-hand. Bellagio and MGM Grand both publish a 72-hour window: the first night's deposit is fully refundable if Room Reservations is told at least 72 hours before arrival, and a late cancellation or no-show forfeits it. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe advertise 'FREE 48-Hour Cancellation*' with an asterisk whose condition is not printed on the page read. The Venetian and Palazzo publish no poker-rate deadline, but do publish that prepaid offers are non-cancellable and non-refundable unless the offer says otherwise, and that checking out early costs $100 plus tax. Wynn publishes deposit terms only (one night charged at booking) and no refund window.
These are general terms, not proof that a particular offer is refundable. Check the exact rate's deadline and deposit before booking; the Venetian poker-rate deadline is not established here.bellagio.mgmresorts.com
F-07TaxesRoom-tax treatment must be verified at checkout
The Venetian's hotel terms read on 2026-11-19 state a 13.38% room tax. A previous audit cited a Clark County guide and noted its old internal date (F-15). Other hotels' individual tax rates and the treatment of each fee are not assumed from that figure.
The Venetian-only scenario uses 13.38% as an illustrative input, not a verified checkout tax bill.www.clarkcountynv.gov
F-08WalkingWalking times are estimates, not measured door-to-seat durations

F-11HotelsA per-night rate is not the bill: occupancy and early-departure charges
The properties charge more than the nightly rate and resort fee in two ways a rate comparison misses. The Venetian and Palazzo add $50 per night for each additional guest 13 or older above double occupancy, and $100 plus tax for checking out before the scheduled departure date. Bellagio adds $50 plus tax per occupant per night above two. MGM Grand adds $40 plus tax per occupant per night above two. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe do not publish an occupancy charge on the page read.
Include any applicable extra-occupant and early-departure charges in the exact-stay checkout comparison; property terms do not establish a current room quote.www.venetianlasvegas.com
