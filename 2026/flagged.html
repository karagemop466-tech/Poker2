Flagged for review

Every value that could not be confirmed from an official source, and every irregularity found in an official document. Nothing here blocks the site — it is the list of things you should look at yourself, with the link to do it.

Two of these are defects in the official documents, not gaps in our reading. X-01: the official schedule PDF prints a sponsor banner where the date should be on twelve rows. X-02: the official series overview still calls an ended series its “Current Series”.

F-01Entry rulesNo Venetian structure sheet states a single-entry or freezeout rule
All 37 official structure sheets were read on 2026-11-17. Not one contains the words 'single entry', 'one entry', 'freezeout' or 'no re-entry'. Every one-day sheet is simply silent on re-entry, rebuy and add-on. Silence is not a prohibition, so no event is marked 'single-entry confirmed'.
The single-entry filter can only offer 'no re-entry/add-on clause printed', never 'confirmed'.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-02Entry rulesTwo events price a second buy-in on the official sheet
Events 18 and 58 ($200 Valuetown Bounty) print: 'PokerAtlas PRO players will receive a twenty ($20) rake discount on their initial $200 buy in only ... All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200.' Pricing a subsequent buy-in means more than one buy-in per player is contemplated.
Events 18 and 58 are moved out of the single-entry candidate group.www.venetianlasvegas.com
X-01Official document defectThe official schedule PDF has a garbled date column on 12 rows
In dse-nov-2026-schedule.pdf the DATE cell on 12 rows prints a sponsor banner ('@PokerAtlas PRO', '@PokerAtlas* TOUR') or a series logo ('LIPS CHAMPIONSHIP ...') instead of the date. Those rows are events 17, 18, 32, 33, 35, 36, 39, 40, 46, 47 (1A), 49 (1A and 1B) and 58.
Every affected date was recovered from the official HTML schedule page and is marked 'date from HTML' in the row detail. The PDF alone is not sufficient to place those events.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-03Prize poolsBounty guarantees include bounty money, so they are not like-for-like
Bounty and PKO sheets state the guarantee 'consists of the actual prize pool plus the total amount of bounties that are awarded'. A $8,000 bounty guarantee is therefore a smaller cash prize pool than a $8,000 non-bounty guarantee.
Bounty events are shown in a separate group and are never mixed into the like-for-like ranking.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-04ScheduleNo official finish time is published for any event
The schedule gives start time, registration close, chips and level length, but no end time. A finish time cannot be derived without a field size, which the organiser does not publish in advance.
Overlap flags are computed as 'same-day entries cannot both be played from the start'. The later event is offered as a conditional fallback whose deadline is its official registration close. Nothing is presented as a guaranteed finish.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-05HotelsNo dated room quote was captured
Official booking engines render rates with client-side JavaScript, so no dated checkout total, nightly average or rate timestamp could be read on 2026-11-17. The Venetian's own page also says future poker rates 'are only provided out to a maximum of 150 days from the current date' and must be booked by phone.
The true-cost calculator needs you to enter the nightly rate you are quoted; it is not prefilled.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-06HotelsNo cancellation deadline published on the pages read
None of the official hotel pages read states a cancellation cutoff for a flexible rate.
The cancellation column stays blank. Confirm it before any non-refundable payment.www.venetianlasvegas.com
F-07TaxesRoom tax rate is sourced to the county, not re-read this session
13.38% comes from Clark County's own 'General Information Guide for Transient Lodging Establishments' and is corroborated by the Venetian's hotel terms. It was read on 2026-11-16 by an earlier session and was not re-read today.
Used as the calculator default. Re-check on the county page before you book.www.clarkcountynv.gov
F-08WalkingWalk times are routing estimates, not a Google or Apple Maps measurement
The walk matrix was computed from OpenStreetMap data with the OSRM foot profile (routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot) on 2026-11-16. A Google Maps walking-directions link is published on every row so you can confirm each pair yourself.
The 30-minute filter is applied to the routed estimate and is labelled as an estimate.www.openstreetmap.org
F-09Other venuesWynn's poker page is currently unavailable
https://www.wynnlasvegas.com/casino/poker-room returned a not-found page on 2026-11-17. Wynn findings on this site are carried from an earlier session's reading of the official WPT structures PDF on 2026-11-16 and are labelled as such.
Wynn rows are marked 'cited, not re-read' and are not presented as current.www.wynnlasvegas.com
F-10Other venuesOnly The Venetian publishes a dated schedule plus a per-event structure sheet
Every other Strip room checked either publishes no tournament schedule, no structure sheet, or both. Horseshoe publishes six daily start times with no buy-ins; Resorts World's poker page redirects to a dining page; Westgate publishes room hours only; MGM's weekly grid carries no year; Bellagio's Saturday sheet contradicts itself.
The ranked tables are Venetian DSE IV only. That is a coverage limit, not a preference.www.caesars.com
X-02Official page defectThe Venetian series overview still labels an ended series as 'Current Series'
The DeepStack overview page shows 'Current Series: WPT Fall Festival, October 28 - November 15, 2026' even though today is 2026-11-17 and the live series is DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 - Dec 23, 2026).
Do not rely on that page's 'Current Series' label; use the dated event page and schedule PDF.www.venetianlasvegas.com
