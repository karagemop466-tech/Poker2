Las Vegas single-entry poker trip planner

Every tournament, hotel and walking route on this site is transcribed from an official venue document that was read line by line and is linked from every row. Where the organiser publishes nothing, the cell is empty and the gap is logged. No value on this site is inferred.

The headline finding: no Strip poker room publishes a single-entry rule. All 37 official Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV structure sheets were read on 2026-11-17. Not one contains a one-entry-only clause. What they do let you do is separate the events whose sheet is silent on re-entry, rebuy and add-on from the ones that expressly price a second buy-in, run multiple flights, award bounties, or pay out in seats.

What is on this site

Tournaments

  • Tournaments — all 64 events, searchable, with a date filter and a single-entry toggle.
  • Entry rules — single-entry, re-entry, add-on and fee fields stored separately, each with its source excerpt and retrieval date.
  • Day plan — one primary event per day plus a conditional fallback, with overlap flags built from official start times and registration closes.

Stay and cost

  • Hotels — resort fees, tax, parking and a dated rate log, plus a true-cost-per-night calculator.
  • Walking times — every hotel × poker-room pair with a 30-minute filter and a Google Maps link per row.
  • Trip cost — entries × buy-ins + nights × true nightly cost.

Decide

  • Executive summary — the important details on one page.
  • Questions — what must be answered before any decision.
  • Suggestions — options to choose between before anything changes.

Verify

  • Flagged for review — every unverified value and irregularity.
  • Sources — the source register and the scheduled re-check.
  • Repo review — what the repository contains, line by line.

Data policy. Every value on this site is transcribed from an official venue document that was read and is linked on every row. A value the organiser does not publish is left empty and listed on Flagged for review. Nothing is inferred, estimated or filled in from memory. Sources retrieved 2026-11-17.

This is a research tool, not a booking service. Re-confirm every official page before you pay anything non-refundable. This /2026/ surface is generated by scripts/build2026.py; the site root hosts the older multi-venue dashboard, and the two do not share a generator.
