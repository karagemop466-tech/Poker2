Sources & verification method

Research snapshot dated 16 November 2026 (UTC). Every event row in the site links to the relevant official casino/event page or official schedule/structure PDF. Hotel rows link to the relevant operator hotel, booking or fee source. The source links are provided for manual review.

Verification standard No third-party tournament aggregators or hotel-fee trackers were used to populate entries or fee values. If an official page did not confirm a specific value, the site leaves it blank or marks it unverified. Arithmetic is labeled as calculation, never as an operator quote.

How figures were handled

FieldRuleMeaning here
Guarantee multiplePublished guaranteed amount ÷ published buy-in.Calculated arithmetic only, rounded to one decimal; not probability, equity, profit, or expected return.
Blank amountOfficial source does not publish the value in the linked event data checked.Blank means unknown, not $0, “none,” or unavailable.
Entry statusOnly mark verified if official event-specific terms clearly establish single-entry/no-extra-spend.All listed event status remains “Unverified”; this is intentionally conservative.
Hotel totalRequire date-specific operator quote with taxes and mandatory fees.No exact total is asserted. Two Venetian illustrative floor calculations show their assumption and are explicitly not quotes.
Walk distanceRequire official route or direct property confirmation.On-property pairings are described; measured minutes left blank.
