A log, never a single overwritten value

Rate log

Every rate observation is its own row, with the date it was observed, what it is (a published starting rate, a mandatory fee, a tax rate or a dated quote), the stay dates it applies to, the occupancy basis and the source. Nothing here is ever overwritten, so a rate that changes shows up as a second row rather than silently replacing the first.

Why a log instead of a rate column

A single "rate" cell conflates four different things that look alike and behave differently: a published starting rate, a mandatory fee, a tax percentage and a real dated quote. Overwriting one with another loses the distinction, and the distinction is the whole decision. The $119 Venetian figure is a starting rate conditional on six hours of play per night; a $174 checkout total for 6–10 December would be a quote. They belong in the same log and must never occupy the same cell.

The log also gives you the audit trail for a dispute: if a property changes a fee, the earlier observation stays visible with its own date and its own link.

How to append a real quote

  1. Open the property's own booking page in a browser (not a script) for the exact arrival and departure dates and the exact occupancy.
  2. Walk to the final checkout screen. Capture: the room subtotal, the nightly average, the mandatory resort fee line, the tax line, any parking line, the cancellation deadline, and the timestamp of the capture.
  3. Append it to data/poker/dataset.json under rates with kind: "dated_quote", the stay_dates, the occupancy, and the URL you read it from. Run python3 scripts/build_dashboard.py.
  4. Compare like-for-like: the same dates, the same occupancy, the same tax treatment. A prepaid non-refundable total is not comparable to a flexible rate, so record the cancellation terms on the row.

Nothing in this repository can do step 1 for you — every booking engine read returned placeholders to a non-browser client, which is recorded as flag F-03.

Verification rule for this site. Every table row links to the official page, PDF or structure sheet the value came from, and shows the date that source was last read (2026-11-16). A cell showing — means no official source stated the value. Nothing is inferred or filled in. Guarantee ÷ entry fee is a descriptive ratio, not a forecast of winnings.
