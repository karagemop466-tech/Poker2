Results Home Resort Casino Poker DeepStack Poker Series Tournaments DeepStack Extravaganza Poker Series The series that started it all back in 2007. The DeepStack Extravaganza became a reality at the request of players seeking better value and more opportunities to hone and develop their skills. By putting the players first, The Venetian Poker Room developed these DeepStack poker tournaments. This poker series provides players with more starting chips, a more favorable structure, and longer levels. DeepStack Extravaganza poker tournaments happen five times a year and are a must on every poker player’s calendar. They provide tremendous value in terms of exciting high-stakes poker play and some of the largest prize pools around. Recreational players, all the way to professionals, sharpen their skills in these Las Vegas poker tournaments. DeepStack Showdown Poker Series events run from one to two weeks at a time. They offer multiple buy-ins each day of varying amounts to appeal to a range of players. The DeepStack Showdown Poker Series is one of the top poker tournaments in Vegas.
