Room Reservation Policies GUARANTEE POLICY A valid credit card is required to confirm your reservation. One night s room rate and tax will be charged at time of booking. RESORT FEE INFORMATION Reservations will be charged a daily resort fee of $55 plus tax. The applicable resort fee will be added to your room account for each complimentary (if applicable) and purchased room night, which entitles you to receive the following: • ◦ Complimentary self-parking for up to two vehicles; • ◦ In-room Wi-Fi of 15 Mbps on an unlimited number of devices; • ◦ Complimentary bottled water, refreshed daily; • ◦ Unlimited local, domestic long distance (including HI and AK) and toll free calls; • ◦ Access for up to two persons to our fitness center; and • ◦ A digital newsstand of magazines and newspapers. Some amenities, such as the fitness center, parking, and pool, are subject to availability and guest demand.
