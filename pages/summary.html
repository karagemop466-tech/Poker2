Executive summary and analysis

Executive summary

  • No event is confirmed single-entry. 38 Venetian 1-day events (Nov 18–Dec 23, 2026) have a structure sheet read with no re-entry language. They are candidates only, consistent with the main site.
  • Largest guarantee among candidates: the $1,100 PKO (Event 52, Dec 15, $75,000 GTD, official schedule).
  • Wynn WPT and most Bellagio events allow re-entry (Wynn: unlimited per flight, Prime: one re-entry). They are kept in the separate spending section.
  • Hotel ranking is partial. Official resort fees are $55 plus tax per night at the Venetian, Palazzo, Wynn, ARIA and Bellagio. The Venetian and Palazzo publish poker suite rates from $119 and $209 per night, before the $55 fee and tax. Those rates require qualifying play each night (six hours of cash, or a $300+ tournament buy-in). Walking distances and dated quotes are not verified.
  • Clark County tax is unverified and left blank.

Analysis

  • Candidate events are concentrated at The Venetian / Palazzo. The cheapest candidates are $200 events at 5:10 PM.
  • The poker suite rate requires a $300+ tournament buy-in each night (or six hours of cash). Candidate $300+ events therefore also satisfy that requirement, subject to the rate terms.
  • For the 8 AM plan, 11:10 AM events (reg closes 4:55 PM) and 5:10 PM events (reg closes 9:40 PM) do not overlap on start time. Event length is unpublished, so overlap cannot be ruled out.
  • Re-entry events carry an advantage for players who can spend more. They are excluded from the candidate list.

Method

Schedule data from the official Venetian DSE IV PDF (fetched 2026-11-16). Structure sheets were used for fees and re-entry language. Wynn values come from its official PDFs read earlier; the Wynn schedule PDF failed on re-fetch. Bellagio values come from its dated PDFs. Aggregator sites were not used.

Data as of 2026-11-16. Every row cites an official source. Blank cells are unverified and are listed on the Flagged page.
