Executive summary and analysis

Executive summary

  • Single-entry field is thin. Only 38 Venetian 1-day events (Nov 18–Dec 23, 2026) had a structure sheet read with no re-entry language. All are on the Strip (The Palazzo poker room). Most are $200–$600 buy-ins.
  • Largest verified guarantee among single-entry events: the $1,100 PKO (Event 52, Dec 15, $75,000 GTD). Top buy-in is $1,100. The Venetian's largest flighted events are on the separate list.
  • Wynn WPT and most Bellagio events allow re-entry (Wynn: unlimited per flight, Prime: one re-entry). They are kept in the separate spending section.
  • Hotel ranking is not possible yet. Official resort fees are $55 plus tax per night at the Venetian, Palazzo, Wynn, ARIA and Bellagio (Gold+ waives for ARIA and Bellagio). Nightly rates and walking distances are not published on official pages.
  • Clark County tax is unverified and left blank.

Analysis

  • Strip single-entry events are concentrated at The Palazzo / Venetian. The 5:10 PM $200 and $300 bounty events are the cheapest single-entry options.
  • For the 8 AM plan, 11:10 AM events (reg closes 4:55 PM) and 5:10 PM events (reg closes 9:40 PM) do not overlap on start time. Event length is unpublished, so overlap cannot be ruled out.
  • Re-entry events carry an advantage for players who can spend more. They are excluded from the single-entry ranking.

Method

Schedule data from the official Venetian DSE IV PDF (fetched 2026-11-16). Structure sheets were used for fees and re-entry language. Wynn values come from its official PDFs read earlier; the Wynn schedule PDF failed on re-fetch. Bellagio values come from its dated PDFs. Aggregator sites were not used.

Data as of 2026-11-16. Every row cites an official source. Blank cells are unverified and are listed on the Flagged page.
