Sample All-Day Schedule — Single Entry Only

Each plan below uses only events from the single-entry table (Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV). Travel between venues is on foot; walking times are estimates — verify on Google Maps.

The 8 AM start cannot be filled with a verified tournament. No tournament in the verified data starts at 8 AM. The earliest verified single-entry start is 10:10 AM (Venetian). Wynn’s earliest daily is 12:00 PM but it allows re-entry (advantage section). So an “8 AM start” means arriving at 8 AM for breakfast, registration, and buy-in — the first verified single-entry event begins at 10:10 AM. See Open Questions.

Building your own day. Pick any single-entry row from the tournament table whose time doesn’t overlap another event you want, walk between Venetian and nearby hotels/venues (all estimated ≤18 minutes), and stack a morning event (10:10 AM or 11:10 AM) with an evening event (5:10 PM–6:10 PM). Afternoon gaps are typical because most verified single-entry events run at 10:10/11:10 AM or 5:10 PM–6:10 PM — a midday block (12:00–2 PM) is the biggest scheduling hole (see Open Questions).
Schedule risks. Events can be cancelled, suspended, or retimed by management without notice (Venetian and Wynn terms both reserve this right). Re-entry within a flight is not confirmed for any Venetian event — read the linked structure sheet before assuming single-entry status. Times are Pacific Time.
