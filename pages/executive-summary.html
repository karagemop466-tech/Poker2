4. Suggested playing plan (single entry only)

  • Anchor hotel: Venetian/Palazzo if you’re playing mostly Venetian events (on site); Wynn/Encore if you want Wynn access too (both are ~12 minutes apart on foot, estimate only).
  • Typical day: arrive 8:00 AM → first verified single-entry event 10:10 AM or 11:10 AM → evening event 5:10 PM–6:10 PM. Afternoons are the scheduling gap (see Open Questions).
  • High-value days: Sat Nov 28 (Events 16/18), Tue Dec 15 (Event 52), Fri Nov 27 / Wed Dec 23 (Event 14/63).
  • Avoid Wynn daily tournaments and Venetian multi-flight events if you insist on single entry only — they are in the advantage section for a reason.

5. Bottom line

Best single-entry value on verified data: Venetian Event 18 ($200, $20K GTD, Sat Nov 28, 5:10 PM) at 100x guarantee-to-buy-in; best high-buy-in single entry: Event 52 ($1,100, $75K GTD, Tue Dec 15). Stay at the Venetian/Palazzo or Wynn/Encore to eliminate transit.
