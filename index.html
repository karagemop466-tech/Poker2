The headline, stated plainly

The requested trip cannot be built to specification from official sources today. Three of the requirements collide with what the casinos actually publish:

  • Single-entry only. Every property that publishes an entry rule publishes a re-entry, rebuy or paid add-on. The Venetian's DeepStack sheets are the most promising — they state the fee split, chips, level length and late-registration close, and they block play after a player bags chips — but none of the 237 rows states "one entry only". So the single-entry short-list is empty by design, not by omission.
  • An 8 AM all-day schedule. The earliest verified start on the Strip is 10:00 at Caesars Palace, with the Venetian following at 10:10 AM. Wynn also has 10:00 AM starts, but only on WPT series days. An 8 AM start would leave two hours with nothing to enter.
  • True hotel cost. Resort fees, the 13.38% Clark County tax and the parking schedule are all verified. The room rate is not: every booking engine returned placeholder prices to a non-browser client, so no dated checkout total exists.

What is solid: 237 events with official start times, buy-ins, guarantees and structure links; printed fee splits for 67 of them; a measured hotel→poker-room walking matrix; and a ranked list that separates events where you can spend more from events that stay silent.

A low number means the guarantee needs a modest field; a number near or below the field the room usually draws means the advertised guarantee is likely to be met or beaten.
