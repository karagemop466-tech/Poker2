Findings

Single-entry confirmation is now sheet-level

All 37 Venetian DSE IV structure sheets were read in full. 46 one-day (plus one two-day) events contain no re-entry, re-buy or add-on clause and are confirmed under the site's documented standard. The two Valuetown events are held back on their 'subsequent buy in's' wording.

Wynn WPT is a re-entry series

Every WPT event whose terms are published in the official structures document allows re-entry (unlimited per flight until level 9–10, or one re-entry per flight for WPT Prime). The 12 documented event types are excluded from the single-entry list. 33 schedule rows have no published terms and stay unverified.

Value ranking (descriptive ratio)

Ranked by guarantee ÷ buy-in among confirmed events with a printed guarantee: Event 36 $800 bounty $75K (93.75×), Event 54 $600 bounty $50K (83.33×), Event 52 $1,100 PKO $75K (68.18×). Bounty guarantees include bounty money — read the ratio as description, not EV.

Plan one seat per day, evenings conditional

For Dec 6–13 the plan gives each day a primary confirmed event and, where a second exists, a conditional evening fallback. 1-Day events run to a winner, so back-to-back seats are flagged as overlapping.

Walkability

Venetian/Palazzo are on-site for DSE IV. Harrah's/LINQ/Flamingo/Cromwell are 9–17 min from the Venetian. Wynn/Encore guests are 0–13 min from the WPT room (in Encore). Golden Nugget (83 min), Westgate (39 min), Mandalay Bay (52 min) fail the 30-minute rule to the Venetian.

Hotel economics

Verified: Venetian/Palazzo/Wynn/Encore resort fee $55+tax; MGM Grand $50+tax; ARIA/Bellagio $55+tax; conditional Venetian poker rates from $119/$209 per night; 13.38% room tax; Venetian parking $20/day. Not verified: dated room quotes, cancellation deadlines, parking elsewhere — all blank and flagged.

How to use this planner

  1. Fix dates & budget — already set above (adjustable in the trip calculator).
  2. Filter to single-entry events — Tournaments → toggle “Confirmed single-entry only”. Events with any spend-more mechanic sit in their own section.
  3. Compare — guarantee ÷ entry-fee ratio (descriptive, not a forecast), stack, level length and day fit. Bounty/flight events are labeled.
  4. Hotels — Hotels & rates for the dated rate log and true cost/night formula; Walking times for the ≤30-minute test with Google Maps confirmation links.
  5. Day plan — Day plan gives each day a primary event and a conditional evening fallback with overlap flags.
  6. Reconfirm before paying — the Monitor re-checks every official URL daily and flags changed sources; re-verify schedules and rates before any nonrefundable payment.
