What an all-day schedule can—and cannot—do

8:00 AMNo verified tournament start in source reviewed.

11:10 AMMultiple daytime entries on some dates; often overlap or are day-2 restarts, not additional fresh events.
5:10 PM+Some evening events overlap with likely long-running daytime fields; no reliable same-day multi-entry plan without published blind levels / late-reg cutoff and expected duration.

Do not assume a player can finish one event and late-register another. Multi-day flights (1A/1B etc.) are separate entry days for one tournament, not necessarily additional tournaments; avoid treating them as a one-day schedule.

Decision-support improvements

01

Strict entry-rule gate

Require official structure/registration citation per event. Include only clearly one-entry events in “Eligible”; send re-entry/rebuy/add-on, bounty, and unclear rules to a separately labeled spending-advantage section.

Proposed · not implemented
02

All-in hotel cost calculator

For exact dates and occupancy, capture checkout total, nightly average, mandatory resort fee and tax, parking, cancellation deadline, and rate timestamp. Compare like-for-like.

Proposed · not implemented
03

Conflict-aware itinerary

Use start time, late-registration cutoff and plausible event duration to flag overlapping entries; give each day a primary event and a conditional fallback, not an impossible sequence.

Proposed · not implemented
04

Comparable tournament value

Rank only after confirming total entry fee and guarantee. Display guaranteed pool ÷ entry fee as a descriptive ratio—not a forecast—and label bounty/flight events separately.
