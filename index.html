How to read this site

  • Sheet silent on re-entry — the official structure sheet was read in full and prints no re-entry, rebuy or add-on mechanism. It also never states “no re-entry”: this is silence, not a published freezeout rule, so no event on this site is labelled confirmed single-entry (see flag IR-8234-02).
  • Entry limit ambiguous — sheet read, but wording suggests possible extra buy-ins.
  • Spend-more — a verified way to buy more chips or entries. Kept out of the main ranking.
  • UNVERIFIED — the value or rule could not be confirmed from an official source. Left blank, never inferred.

Pool ÷ fee figures are descriptive ratios of published numbers only, not forecasts of value or ROI.
