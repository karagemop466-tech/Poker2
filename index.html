Value signal (not a prediction)

A guarantee-to-buy-in ratio can help sort events, but field size, fee/rake, attendance, and structure matter. No ranking is asserted until those inputs and single-entry eligibility are confirmed. A guarantee is not a guaranteed individual payout.

Separate review: formats where added spend or format affects comparison

Official schedule names bounty / progressive knockout events and multi-flight events. These are kept out of the strict single-entry shortlist because the schedule alone does not establish whether rebuy, re-entry, add-ons, or other additional spending is allowed. Bounty components are part of the listed buy-in structure, not proof of optional add-on. Check official event structure sheets before classifying.

Questions and information needed before a sound decision

  1. Vacation dates and number of nights? Needed for upcoming event eligibility and true hotel pricing.
  2. Number of guests / rooms? Needed for comparable hotel quotes and taxes.
  3. Maximum tournament budget per day and total? The buy-in is not total bankroll exposure if re-entry is permitted.
  4. Does “single entry” mean exactly one bullet, or is one entry per starting flight acceptable? Multi-flight rules vary.
  5. Are bounty/PKO formats acceptable? They have materially different prize mechanics.
  6. Would you accept tournaments that start after 8 AM? The reviewed Venetian schedule begins at 10:10 AM earliest; a full-day chain needs finish/late-registration estimates.
  7. Maximum walking distance / mobility constraints? “30 minutes” should be checked as a route, not straight-line distance; resort interiors can add substantial time.
  8. What hotel quality floor and cancellation flexibility? Both change the value comparison.

These are decision inputs, not a request for manual work in this session; missing values are left unfilled as requested.

Suggestions to improve decision-making

  1. Maintain event-level records with date, local start, buy-in, guarantee, structure PDF, entry limit, re-entry, rebuy, add-on, late registration, estimated duration and last verified timestamp.
  2. Label entry-format evidence as confirmed / unknown, and exclude unknown events from a strict single-entry filter by default.
  3. Rank eligible tournaments using buy-in, guaranteed pool, guarantee-to-buy-in ratio, structure, fee, field estimate, duration and walk; never treat the guarantee ratio as expected return.
  4. Use date-specific official hotel checkout totals including mandatory resort fees/taxes and divide by nights; preserve cancellation terms and capture timestamp.
  5. Calculate walking routes between hotel and poker room using official venue addresses and a route provider, with caveat for casino interior distance.
  6. Build a schedule only after late registration end, average/maximum duration, overlap, and travel buffer are verified; a calendar start time alone cannot prove a playable all-day itinerary.
  7. Provide source links on every row, source check timestamp, change alert, and an explicit flag queue for ambiguous or conflicting data.
