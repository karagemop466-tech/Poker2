Build a trip schedule you can actually play

There is not enough confirmed information to produce an all-day tournament itinerary starting at 8 AM. This page shows what must be resolved and proposes site improvements without changing the research rules.

Questions that block a reasonable decision

1. What are the travel dates, and how many nights?Needed for currently published schedules, room quotes, stay total, and whether Nov/Dec 2026 events are relevant. Official hotel rates change by date.
2. What is the maximum event buy-in and total daily poker budget?Without a cap, “best” by affordability cannot be ranked; a larger guarantee usually comes with a larger entry price.
3. Is a tournament acceptable only if the official rules explicitly prohibit re-entry, re-buy, re-entry flights, and add-ons?Strict single-entry means one paid entry per participant/event, and no optional spend to re-enter or add on. Tournament title or “1Day” schedule label alone does not establish it.
4. Which poker formats are acceptable?NLH-only versus mixed games, bounty/PKO, turbo, seniors/ladies eligibility restrictions, satellites, and multi-flight/Day 2 events change eligibility and fit.
5. What hotel quality floor and room/guest needs apply?Room type, occupancy, refundable requirement, parking/car, accessibility, and loyalty/member rates affect all-in cost.
6. Does “all day starting 8 AM” mean a game must begin at 8, or simply begin playing by then?The upcoming sourced Venetian schedule starts at 10:10 AM earliest. No verified 8 AM tournament appears in reviewed official schedules.
7. Can the itinerary use cash games, or tournaments only?Cash play could bridge tournament gaps, but does not satisfy the stated goal of entering as many tournaments and may be required by a conditional poker-room rate.

Schedule recommendation (provisional, not bookable)

For Nov 18–26, investigate Venetian one-day NLH candidates first, especially Nov 18 ($300/$10K GTD) and Nov 25 ($400/$15K GTD), subject to the entry-rule check. Published starts run 10:10 or 11:10 AM, so an 8 AM tournament day is not supported by this schedule. Avoid planning a second event based on a predicted finish: official schedule rows provide no guaranteed end times. Day 2 restart sessions do not count as new entries.

There is no substantiated multi-venue, same-day tournament chain yet. Before itinerary selection, cross-check official Wynn and MGM calendars for that exact date and ask each room about late registration, overlaps, and re-entry/add-ons.

Sources: Venetian official series calendar, Wynn official poker page, MGM Grand official poker page.
