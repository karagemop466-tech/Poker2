Separate review: spend-more / format advantage risk

Venetian event listing includes bounty/PKO formats (Nov 23–24) and later bounty events. These are kept out of any pure single-entry recommendation. A bounty prize does not itself prove add-ons or re-entry, but tournament entry and side-bounty structures can change the economics; verify the full structure sheet. Multi-flight events such as Event 1 are not listed as fresh one-day entries here, because the schedule gives multiple starting flights and a Day 2 restart.

Other Strip daily schedules surfaced official dates/times but not entry fees or guaranteed prize pools in the official pages reviewed: MGM Grand lists daily 11 AM, 7 PM, and 11 PM tournaments; Wynn publishes general daily events and links to the current calendar. Neither has been presented as a verified single-entry/value candidate here.
