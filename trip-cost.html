The formula and the true-cost definition

Total = (entries × buy-in) + (nights × room rate) + (nights × resort fee) + (room rate + resort fee) × tax rate × nights + (parking days × daily parking) + other.

True cost per night = room rate + mandatory resort fee + tax on both. Taxes are applied to the resort fee as well as the room rate, because the Clark County room tax applies to the total room charge — the Venetian's own terms state the 13.38% room tax and the $55-plus-tax fee separately, and the fee is taxable.

Inputs

Defaults: $119 is the Venetian's published poker starting rate — conditional on six hours of live cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in per night, and not a dated quote. $55 is the Venetian resort fee. 13.38% is the Clark County room tax printed on the Venetian's hotel terms. $20/day is the Venetian's registered-guest self-parking rate. Change the room rate to a real quote the moment you have one.

What the calculator refuses to do

  • It will not guess a room rate. The $119 default is a published starting rate, and it is conditional. Every booking engine read in this session returned a $XXX placeholder to a non-browser client, so no dated total exists.
  • It will not add the extra-occupant charge. Both MGM properties charge $40–$50 plus tax per night above two occupants. If you are travelling as a pair, that line is zero; as a group, add it to Other.
  • It will not model the poker rate's condition. The $119 rate requires six hours of live cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in per night, else the rate reverts to the prevailing rate. If you plan rest days, use the public rate instead.
  • It will not count a "voluntary" gratuity as optional. Several events sell chips for a gratuity (Bellagio $40 for +15,000; MGM Grand $11 for +10,000). Declining it puts you at a real chip disadvantage, so treat it as part of the entry cost where the fields are competitive.
