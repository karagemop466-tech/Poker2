| Sheets that print an explicit one-entry-only rule | 0 |

**The distinction that matters:** a structure sheet that never mentions re-entry is not
evidence that re-entry is forbidden. Silence is not a prohibition, and this register does not
treat it as one. Venetian one-day rows are therefore labelled *sheet silent on re-entry*, kept in
their own list apart from the spend-more (re-entry / add-on) events, and flagged as needing a
written answer from the poker room before any entry decision depends on them. The counter-example
is Wynn: its WPT structures PDF states re-entry in writing (the $600 event allows unlimited
re-entry per flight until the start of level 9), so Wynn events are classified as spend-more on
the strength of a printed rule, not of silence.

## Consistency checks run on this register

* `scripts/audit.py` re-derives, for every Venetian row on the site: the sheet it cites exists in
  this registry, the sheet covers that event number, the house fee matches, the chips match, the
  printed late-registration wording matches, the clock close of registration matches the printed
  time, and the guarantee does not contradict the sheet.
* `scripts/check_links.py` confirms every PDF link here resolves to an official Venetian host.
* Event numbers and dates are the schedule page's, not the sheets' — the sheets print one
  structure shared by several numbered events, so a mismatch would mean the schedule page and the
  PDF disagree. None was found.

* Sheet 25 (turbo milestone satellite) prints "6 levels" with **no clock time** for the close of
  registration, so its three rows carry a blank close time rather than an invented one.
* Several sheets cover many one-day events with different guarantees (sheet 7: $30,000 / $40,000;
  sheet 11: $30,000 / $40,000 / $50,000). The guarantee is taken from the event's own schedule row
  where the sheet prints more than one figure.

  events (18, 58) are labelled *entry limit ambiguous*, not single-entry.
* Sheets 31 and 32 describe LIPS events with eligibility conditions (female, or female/male team)
  and add a flat LIPS fee on top of the house fee; the fee split here is the sheet's own.
