Findings

One venue has a dated, per-event official source set - and it drives everything

The Venetian's DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 - Dec 23, 2026) publishes 64 numbered events as 94 rows with a structure sheet per event. No other Strip room in this dataset publishes a comparable dated document set, so the ranked table is Venetian-only and that limit is stated on every page rather than hidden.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com
45 one-day events carry no re-entry, rebuy or add-on clause on their sheet - and every sheet has now been read

All 37 structure sheets were read, not ten. 45 one-day rows show a late-registration window and no additional-buy-in provision: $200 RaceDay Stack, $200/$300/$400/$600 no-limit hold'em, bounties, PLO, Big O and the LIPS events, at 10:10, 11:10, 12:10 and 17:10 starts. 41 of them have both the fee and the guarantee confirmed, so they can be ranked. Zero rows in the dataset are still labelled unverified. The caveat has not changed: not one sheet says 'one entry per player', so these are candidates and not confirmations (FG-02).

The room does write re-entry rules when they exist - which makes the silence meaningful

The PokerAtlas Valuetown sheet (events 18 and 58) states: 'PokerAtlas PRO players will receive a twenty ($20) rake discount on their initial $200 buy in only... All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200.' Two consequences: those events allow further buy-ins, and a paid third-party membership lowers the first entry, which is a spending-based edge. Both are excluded from the single-entry table and shown separately.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com
Multi-flight main events are not single-entry, by their own rule

Event 01's sheet says each starting flight plays to about 12.5% of the field, Day 2 combines 1A-1D, and 'Any player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a subsequent flight' - so an eliminated player may buy into another flight. All 28 flight rows (including the $800/$500K, $1,100/$150K, $1,100/$500K, $2,200/$1M and Seniors events) are therefore in the separate list, not the ranked one.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com
A 'guarantee to buy-in' number worth using, and its limit

The size ratio for the best verified rows: $600 into a $50,000 guarantee (83.3x, event 54, Dec 16), $600 into $40,000 (66.7x, events 28 and 30), $300 into $15,000 (50.0x, nine events). For bounty events the published guarantee explicitly includes total bounties, so the cash-only figure is smaller. The ratio describes the published guarantee per entry; it is not an expected value, and no field size is knowable in advance.

The hotel answer is currently a formula, not a price - because the room rate is the only missing term

Verified from official pages: Venetian/Palazzo $55 resort fee plus tax and 13.38% Clark County room tax, $20/day guest parking, $150/night incidental authorization, $50/night per additional guest aged 13+, $100 plus tax for early checkout, and poker rates 'starting as low as $119 and $209'. Park MGM: $50 fee plus tax, $40 plus tax per extra occupant, $20/day parking, $100/day incidentals, 72-hour refundable first-night deposit. Wynn: $55 plus tax with free self-parking for two vehicles bundled in, $50 extra-guest surcharge, full-stay deposit at booking. Every booking engine renders date-specific prices only in JavaScript, so no nightly rate could be captured; the true cost is exact for fees and tax and open only on the rate. Update 2026-11-17: two itemised dated quotes were captured from the Venetian's own booking engine, so one property now has a real price rather than a formula. Nine of the ten hotels still do not.

The cheapest entries quietly break the cheapest room rate

The Venetian poker suite rate requires, each night, six hours of active live cash play or a $300-or-higher tournament buy-in, completed before 2 AM on the last night - otherwise 'your nightly suite rate will be adjusted to the prevailing rate'. A trip built from $200 RaceDay and $200 bounty events therefore forfeits the $119 rate. The plan builder prefers $300+ primaries for exactly this reason, and flags any day where it cannot.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com
The walking test now returns a real shortlist

45 of 70 hotel-to-venue pairs have a walking duration and 30 of them are 30 minutes or less. Staying at The Venetian puts its own poker room at zero minutes and reaches Wynn (13 min), Caesars Palace (19.5 min) and Horseshoe (20.5 min) on foot, so the whole centre-Strip corridor is one venue cluster. Bellagio is 27.6 minutes - inside the limit but a real walk. MGM Grand is 36.5 minutes and fails the test. Durations are routed estimates, not measurements, and every row links to Google and Apple Maps so you can check it.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com · wynnlasvegas.com · caesars.com

Event #39 is a two-day event, not a multi-flight one - and it is women-only

Reading sheet 32 corrected a classification. The $800 LIPS Ladies Main Event has a single Day 1 (10:10 AM, 10 Dec) and resumes at 10:00 AM on 11 Dec, with the PokerGO-streamed final table at about 7 PM that evening. There are no 1A/1B flights, so it is not a re-entry event - but it occupies a second morning, so it is excluded from a one-day plan. It is also restricted to female players (rule 02), as are the two $100 satellites into it.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com
Three events have an age or gender gate you can fail after paying

The $600 Seniors events (#24, #32) and the $1,100 Seniors (#49) require players to be 50 or older, and the sheets say plainly that anyone younger who buys in is 'not entitled to any prize money or a refund of their buy in'. The LIPS Ladies events are restricted to female players. These were not on the site before this pass; they are now on every row that carries one.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com · venetianlasvegas.com · venetianlasvegas.com
A real hotel quote exists now, and it exposes a three-night price spike

The Venetian's booking engine publishes an itemised breakdown, so two dated quotes were captured on 2026-11-17. Seven nights from 18 Nov 2026: room $3,594.75 + resort fee $385.00 + tax $532.51 = $4,512.26, or $644.61 per night all-in. But the nights are not even - Sun 18 is $164.25, Thu 19 is $614.25, Fri 20 is $1,011.75, Sat 21 is $1,311.75, then $164.25 for each of Sun 22, Mon 23 and Tue 24. Three nights carry most of the week. Both quotes imply a tax of 13.3804%, which independently confirms the published Clark County rate and proves the resort fee is taxed too.

Sources: venetianlasvegas.com · venetianlasvegas.com
Every other Strip room either re-enters, add-ons, or sells chips

Reading the other rooms' official documents settled it. Caesars Palace runs four dailies with a $50 add-on and re-entry after an early elimination. Bellagio's three weeklies allow re-entry through level 8 and sell an extra 15,000-20,000 chips through a 'voluntary' gratuity. Wynn's dailies allow re-entry (or, on Fridays, unlimited rebuys) and a $100 add-on. Golden Nugget allows unlimited re-entry and two add-ons, and says the first add-on does not even go into the prize pool. Not one of them is single-entry, which is why the ranked table is Venetian-only and says so.

Sources: caesars.com · assets.contentstack.io · cdn.wynnresorts.com · goldennugget.com
Wynn's dailies may not run during your trip at all

All four Wynn daily sheets say the event 'may be suspended during Seniors Events, Classic Events, or other Tournament Series', and the WPT World Championship takes over the same poker room from 27 November to 21 December 2026. That covers most of the Venetian series window, so no Wynn daily should anchor a day without a phone call.
