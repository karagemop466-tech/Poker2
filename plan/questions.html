Open questions, and what is already answered

The decision cannot be closed on published data alone. Each question states what is already answered from official sources, what is missing, and exactly who to ask. 'Blocks' refers to the six-step decision sequence on the Summary page.

Q-01 · What are the exact trip dates and how many nights? needs you step 1

What we already haveThe only dated, published schedule in scope is The Venetian's DeepStack Extravaganza IV, November 16 - December 23, 2026 (94 published rows). Any date outside that window has no verified tournament anchor at all.
What is missingYour arrival and departure dates. Every rate, availability and cancellation answer depends on them.
How to close itYou decide. Once fixed, the booking deep links on the Hotels page and the rate log carry them, and the checker compares your checkout total against the modelled cost.
Blockseverything downstream: hotel quote, walking test, room-rate eligibility

Q-02 · What is the nightly budget ceiling and the quality floor? needs you step 1

What we already haveOne official rate statement exists: Venetian/Palazzo poker suite rates 'start as low as $119 and $209', with a $55 resort fee and a 13.38% county room tax. Park MGM publishes a $50 resort fee plus tax and $20/day guest parking. That is enough to build two cost bands, not to pick a winner.
What is missingYour ceiling per night and your minimum acceptable quality tier; no hotel publishes a rating on its own pages (FG-12).
How to close itAnswer here, then choose a rating source (suggestion S-04) so quality becomes a comparable field instead of an adjective.
Blockshotel shortlist

Q-14 · Who is coming, and does sharing change the cost? needs you step 1

What we already haveOccupancy economics: the Venetian's rate is quoted on single/double occupancy and adds $50/night per guest aged 13+; Park MGM adds $40 plus tax per occupant above two; Wynn adds $50 per night above the room's stated capacity and caps standard rooms at four people ($500 per person beyond the cap, up to $3,000).
What is missingParty size. Two players splitting a $119+fee room halves the lodging cost per player and doubles the qualifying-play obligation, since play from all registered guests counts toward the requirement.
How to close itDecide the party; the trip calculator has an occupancy control that applies the surcharge lines automatically.
Blocksthe per-player cost comparison between hotels

Q-15 · Do your dates avoid the 19-21 November 2026 price spike? needs you step 1

What we already haveIn the 7-night quote captured 2026-11-17 the Venetian room rate is $164.25 on Sun 18 Nov, then $614.25, $1,011.75 and $1,311.75 on Thu 19 - Sat 21, then back to $164.25 for Sun 22, Mon 23 and Tue 24. The engine states no reason. Three nights are therefore carrying most of the cost of the week.
What is missingYour dates, and a quote for them.
How to close itQuote a Sunday-Thursday stay and compare it with the same length of stay that includes a Thursday or Friday night. The rate log keeps both so the comparison is like-for-like.
Blockshotel quote

Q-03 · Is the event you want actually single-entry? answered with caveat step 2

What we already haveAll 37 Venetian structure sheets have now been read in full, plus the Bellagio Friday/Saturday/Sunday sheets, the Caesars Palace 2026 daily flyer, the Wynn daily structures PDF, the MGM Grand schedule PDF and the Golden Nugget daily page. 45 Venetian rows carry no rebuy, add-on or re-entry clause and are candidates. NOT ONE official document in this whole dataset contains the sentence 'one entry per player'. The strongest published state is silence, which is why the site says candidate and not confirmed.
What is missingA one-line written answer from the poker room for the specific events you enter.
How to close itEmail lv_cas_poker@venetianlasvegas.com (the address printed on the official poker-rate page) or call 702.414.7657 and ask: 'Is Event #__ strictly one entry per player, with no re-entry, no rebuy and no add-on?' Get it in writing. Until then every candidate row is a candidate.
Blockswhether any event qualifies at all (FG-02)

Q-04 · What is the total cost to enter - is the advertised number fee-inclusive? answered step 2

What we already haveFor the 34 read-sheet events, yes and it is stated per event: e.g. '$58 of the $300 Buy-In is withheld for house fees (Rake - Staff). The prize pool consists of $242 of every $300 Buy In.' So the buy-in is the total you pay; the fee is carved out of it, not added.
What is missingThe PokerAtlas Valuetown events where a PRO member pays $180 instead of $200 for the first entry.
How to close itRead the remaining sheets (the daily job already fetches them) or ask the cage.
Blocksranking, because the guarantee-to-cost ratio changes if the fee is additive

Q-16 · Are you eligible for every event you plan to enter? answered step 2

What we already haveReading the sheets surfaced three eligibility rules the site did not previously carry, and they are now on the rows. The $600 Seniors events (#24, #32) and the $1,100 Seniors (#49) require players to be 50+, and the sheet says a player under 50 who buys in gets no prize money and no refund. The LIPS Ladies events (#33, #37, #39) require female players; #39 is women-only, #35 is female/female or female/male tag teams, and #43 is open to all.
What is missingNothing - this is answered from the sheets.
How to close itFilter the tournaments table and read the Eligibility line under each event.

Q-05 · What does the guarantee actually contain? answered step 3

What we already haveBounty events state it: 'A Prize Pool of $8,000 is guaranteed by the Venetian Poker Room which consists of the actual prize pool plus the total amount of $50 bounties that are awarded.' So the headline guarantee is cash-plus-bounties, not cash to the field.
What is missingNothing for the read sheets; unread ones are marked unknown.
How to close itAlready in the sheet excerpt on each row.
Blockscomparing a $300 bounty event against a $300 flat event

Q-06 · How deep do payouts run, and does that change the ratio's meaning? answered step 3

What we already have'This event pays 12.5% of the field' on every one-day sheet read. Combined with the guarantee that gives a per-cash-place floor you can compute; the site shows guarantee divided by buy-in as a descriptive size ratio only.
What is missingField size, which nobody can know before the event. Any 'expected value' claim would be invented.
How to close itOnly the day's registration count; the results column on the official page stays empty until the event runs.
Blocksturning the ratio into a ranking criterion - it must stay descriptive

Q-07 · Which events can realistically be stacked in one day? answered with caveat step 3

What we already haveRegistration windows and level lengths from the sheets: the 11:10 AM events close late registration at ~4:55 PM, the 5:10 PM events at 9:40 PM, the 10:10 AM RaceDay events at 12:25 PM. So the RaceDay format is the only one that can plausibly finish before an 11:10 AM event; the 5:10 PM event is a late-entry fallback, never a clean second full field.
What is missingActual finish times. No official source publishes average or expected duration, so 'earliest plausible finish' is a derived clock value, labelled as such on the Schedule page.
How to close itTwo live days of observation, or the room's own estimate when you call for Q-03.
Blocksthe 'one event per day, evening only if you can exit' rule

Q-08 · What is the true nightly cost for your stay - itemised? partially answered step 4

What we already haveTwo real dated quotes, captured 2026-11-17 from the Venetian's own booking engine, itemised to the cent. Nov 18-22 2026 (4 nights, Two Queen): room $2,827.00 + resort fee $220.00 + tax $407.69 = $3,454.69, i.e. $863.67 per night all-in. Nov 18-25 2026 (7 nights, King): room $3,594.75 + resort fee $385.00 + tax $532.51 = $4,512.26, i.e. $644.61 per night all-in. Both imply a 13.3804% tax, which independently confirms the published Clark County rate and proves the resort fee is itself taxed.
What is missingA quote for YOUR dates, and any quote at all for Wynn, ARIA, Bellagio, Park MGM, Caesars, Paris, Horseshoe or the Cosmopolitan - those engines build their prices after page load and returned nothing to a static fetch on 2026-11-17.
How to close itOpen the booking link on the Hotels page with your dates, expand 'See full price breakdown', and paste the total into the rate log. Or ring the property and ask for a written quote for your dates that itemises room rate, resort fee, tax and parking.
Blocksany like-for-like hotel comparison (FG-05)

Q-09 · Which cancellation deadline applies to your booking? partially answered step 4

What we already havePark MGM: first night's deposit refundable if cancelled at least 72 hours before arrival. Wynn: full-stay deposit taken at booking; two official offer pages show 48-hour and 72-hour windows. Venetian: deposit at booking (first night or whole stay); prepaid offers are non-cancellable and non-refundable, with no general hour stated.
What is missingThe deadline attached to the specific rate you book - it is offer-coded, not property-wide.
How to close itIt is printed on the reservation confirmation and in the checkout terms; compare it against the tournament start you have committed to.
Blocksthe sequencing rule: confirm schedules before non-refundable payment (step 6)

Q-10 · Is the walk actually under 30 minutes - and does it stay outdoors? partially answered step 4

What we already have45 of 70 hotel-to-poker-room pairs now have a walking duration (OpenStreetMap/OSRM foot routing), and 30 of them are 30 minutes or less. The pairs that matter most: The Venetian to its own poker room 0 min, The Palazzo 6 min, Wynn 13 min, Caesars Palace 19.5 min, Horseshoe 20.5 min, Bellagio 27.6 min. MGM Grand is 36.5 min from The Venetian - over the limit.
What is missingA measured duration rather than a routed estimate, and any duration at all for ARIA, Golden Nugget and The Cosmopolitan. Also missing: whether the route is indoors or on the street, which in Las Vegas heat is a different question from the number of minutes.
How to close itUse the Google/Apple Maps links on the Walking page for each pair, or approve S-06 so the daily job records durations from a routing API.
Blocksthe 30-minute filter, which today correctly returns 'unknown' for every pair (FG-11)

Q-11 · Do the hotel's poker-rate conditions survive your schedule? answered warning step 5

What we already haveThe Venetian poker rate requires, per night, either six hours of active live cash play or a $300-or-higher tournament buy-in, completed before 2 AM on the last night. The $200 RaceDay Stack and the $200 Bounty events do not qualify a night on their own, even though they are the cheapest entries in the series.
What is missingWhether six cash-game hours is realistic between 11:10 AM and 4:55 PM registration - it is not, so the plan must include a $300+ entry every night or accept the prevailing rate.
How to close itYour own schedule: the plan builder already prefers $300+ events for exactly this reason.
Blocksthe difference between a $119 and a prevailing-rate night, which can double the lodging cost

Q-18 · Is a bounty event the right thing to enter at all? answered warning step 5

What we already haveBounty and PKO events are labelled separately on the tournaments table, because the guarantee on them is described by the sheet as 'prize pool + total bounties' - part of the guaranteed number is money you can only win by knocking players out, not by finishing deep. Event #52 ($1,100 PKO, $75K GTD) and #08 ($200 PKO, $8K GTD) work differently again: the bounty on your own head grows as you eliminate people.
What is missingNothing from the sources. The judgement - whether you want variance in the bounty pool - is yours.
How to close itRead the bounty note on each row; the ratio column treats the guarantee the same way for all events, so read the label before you read the number.
Blocksevent selection
