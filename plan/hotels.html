Clark County combined transient lodging tax — 13.38%

Transient Lodging Establishments located within the Primary Gaming Corridor will impose a combined transient lodging tax rate of 13.38 percent. [12.0% (current rate) + 0.5% (LVCVA expansion) + 0.88% (Stadium Construction) = 13.38%]

Scope: Primary Gaming Corridor (the Strip resort corridor). Source: Clark County notice (official).

  • The Venetian (official hotel terms): “Daily rates do not include the applicable Clark County Room Tax of 13.38%.” venetianlasvegas.com
  • Park MGM (official hotel reservation terms): “Lodging taxes apply to the basic room rate, any resort fee, additional per occupant charges, upgrades, as well as any other charges as required by applicable law.” parkmgm.mgmresorts.com
  • Clark County Department of Business License: “Clark County licensee list shows 13.38% Transient Lodging Tax - Resort Hotel (PGC) for Bellagio, Paris Las Vegas, Park MGM, The Cosmopolitan, The LINQ, Flamingo, Harrah's, Cromwell, Sahara and Rio.” clarkcountynv.gov
