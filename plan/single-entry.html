Single-entry candidates, ranked

Ranking uses only rows where the total entry fee and the guarantee are both confirmed from an official structure sheet. Every row below was read in full on 2026-11-16 and contains no rebuy, add-on or re-entry provision. That is not the same as an official one-entry cap — no Venetian sheet publishes one (flag FG-02) — so treat this as the qualifying set produced by the published sources, and get the last sentence confirmed by the room before you rely on it.

How “single-entry” is decided here. A sheet that states a limit is confirmed; a sheet read in full that contains no buy-in clause is a candidate (this list); a sheet not read is excluded from ranking; any rebuy, add-on, priced re-entry, extra flight, or a discount bought with a paid third-party membership goes to the spend-advantage page.
  • single entry candidate — One-day event whose official structure sheet was read in full and contains no rebuy, add-on or re-entry provision. The sheet does NOT state a one-entry cap, so this is the highest state the published sources support; ask the room for a written answer (see Questions).
  • spend advantage — Money buys an edge or extra entries: paid third-party membership discounts, priced subsequent buy-ins, rebuys or add-ons.
  • multi flight — Day 1A/1B/... flights: a player who does not reach Day 2 may buy into another starting flight (stated on the sheet), so the event is not single-entry.
  • unverified — Row exists on the official schedule; the structure sheet has not been read, so entry rules, fee and payout timing are unknown.
  • ratio gtd to buyin — Descriptive only: published guarantee divided by the buy-in. It is not an expected value, a forecast, or an overlay.
