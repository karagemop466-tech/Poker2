Questions that need answers before a decision. 'Open' means the data does not answer it. The 'How to find it' column lists where to look. These are the gaps between what we have verified and what a booking/registration decision needs. Suggestions for discovering the missing information live on the suggestions page.

Q01Which tournaments are single-entry, one-day events with no re-entry, rebuy or add-on? 54 Venetian one-day events are sheet-silent on re-entry, rebuy and add-ons (the strongest position any official document supports); 29 Venetian rows carry a stated entry rule on their sheet — 27 of them a restriction that keeps the event out of a single-entry plan. No Las Vegas room in this dataset publishes “no re-entry”, so no event is labelled confirmed single-entry. 40 rows across Wynn, Horseshoe and MGM have no rule document published at all.Partly answered — from the official sheets, not from the rooms A written one-entry confirmation from each poker room. The structure sheets are now all read (37 of 37), so no further sheet can settle it.
Q02Can a player start play at 8:00 AM with verified single-entry events? No. The earliest verified single-entry start at The Venetian is 11:10 AM. No event in this site starts at 8:00 AM.Answered (no, for these two properties) Check whether any other Strip room runs an 8:00 AM or earlier event.
Q03What is the true nightly cost of each hotel for the user's dates? The mandatory resort fee is $55 plus tax per night at The Venetian, The Palazzo and Wynn, and the room tax is 13.38% for the Strip corridor (13.00% at South Point). What is missing is a room rate: the Venetian poker-rate page prints starting rates of $119 and $209 for a suite 'in either The Venetian or The Palazzo' without saying which property is which, and no dated checkout quote for the trip dates has been captured. True cost per night therefore stays blank rather than being estimated.Partly answered — fees and tax verified, room rate open A dated checkout quote per hotel for the exact stay dates, with resort fee, tax line, parking, cancellation deadline and the time it was read.
Q04Is each hotel within a 30-minute walk of the tournament venue? No casino publishes a walk time, so routing services were used and labelled as such. Google Maps walking directions (read 2026-11-17) and an independent OSRM foot matrix agree on every 30-minute verdict for The Venetian Poker Room: Palazzo 4-6 min, Venetian 5 min, Wynn 12-13 min, Caesars Palace 19-20 min, Horseshoe 21-24 min and Bellagio 28 min are inside the limit; MGM Grand (37-42 min) and ARIA (31-37 min) are outside it. The Wynn poker room is covered by the OSRM matrix alone: Wynn, Encore, Resorts World, Venetian, Palazzo, Westgate, Harrah's, LINQ and Flamingo are inside; Horseshoe, Caesars Palace, Bellagio and everything south of them are outside.Answered as far as routing services allow A second router on the Wynn side, and an in-person check of the two tight calls: Bellagio to the Venetian (28-30 min depending on router) and ARIA to the Venetian (30.6 min by OSRM, 37 min by Google — the only pair where the two routers land on opposite sides of the limit).
