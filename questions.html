Questions that need answers before a decision. 'Open' means the data does not answer it. The 'How to find it' column lists where to look. These are the gaps between what we have verified and what a booking/registration decision needs. Suggestions for discovering the missing information live on the suggestions page.

Q04Is each hotel within a 30-minute walk of the tournament venue? Walking times were NOT verified for any hotel.Open Walking time from each hotel to each tournament venue, from a map or official source.
