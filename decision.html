Decision questions and answers

Each answer is limited to evidence in the linked official sources.

1. Which dates have live tournaments coming up?

Known: The Venetian lists DeepStack Extravaganza IV from Nov 16–Dec 23, 2026; Wynn lists the WPT World Championship Nov 27–Dec 21, 2026. Both primary calendars link from the tournament table.

Still missing: Seat capacity/live availability and whether each event remains unchanged on the day.

2. Which events are definitely single-entry with no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or multiple-flight advantage?

Known: No reviewed event is officially confirmed as “one total entry only.” The Venetian structures checked do not settle re-entry/add-on limits. Wynn's official terms explicitly permit unlimited re-entry per starting flight for its featured $600/$800 events and one re-entry per flight for WPT Prime; those events are excluded into the spend-more section.

Still missing: Written entry-limit rules for other candidate events. The strict single-entry shortlist currently has zero confirmed entries.

3. Are paid add-ons available at any Strip tournament?

Known: Caesars' official guide describes its Caesars Palace $100 daily NLH as a $1,000 guaranteed event with a $50 optional add-on for 15,000 more chips at the first break. That belongs in the separate spend-more section.

Still missing: An event-by-event add-on / rebuy check at every other venue; absence of a mention is not proof of prohibition.

4. Which tournament has the best prize pool for the entry price?

Known: For selected Venetian one-day events, published guarantee ÷ buy-in ranges from 33.3× ($300 / $10,000) to 66.7× ($600 / $40,000). Wynn publishes much larger multi-day guarantees, including $400,000 for a $600 event, $1,000,000 for an $800 event, and $5,000,000 for a $1,100 WPT Prime Championship.

Important: This ratio is not expected value, probability of cashing or profit. It does not account for fees, field size, payout structure, format, multiple entries, or duration. Bounty prize guarantees are not directly comparable.

5. Can one start at 8 AM and play tournaments all day?

Known: No official 8:00 AM start was found. Caesars' guide describes a recurring Horseshoe NLH start at 9:00 AM but lacks a dated listing and entry price/rules. A Venetian $260 satellite is listed at 9:10 AM on Dec 12; direct dated starts reviewed generally begin at 10:10 AM or later.

Still missing: A date-specific Horseshoe schedule and confirmed terms if early starts or an 8 AM start are non-negotiable.

6. Can a player enter a second tournament later the same day?

Known: Some Venetian dates show an 11:10 AM event and a 5:10 PM event. The official schedule supplies start times and some registration-close times.

Still missing: The first event's finish time, remaining registration window, overlap/eligibility policy and whether the player has busted. A start-time plan is not proof that two entries can be played.

7. What is the hotel cost per night for the planned stay?

Known: Venetian and Wynn each publish a $55 nightly resort fee plus tax. Venetian's poker-rate page publishes low starting rates of $119 and $209, but says rates can be higher and require qualifying poker play/availability.

Still missing: Exact dates, room type, occupancy, availability, standard nightly rate, taxes, cancellation terms and total invoice. Exact total remains blank.

8. Are the hotels within a 30-minute walk of a target event?

Known: Staying at the venue's own resort pairs the hotel and poker room/event property (Venetian/Palazzo, Wynn/Encore; Caesars Palace; Horseshoe).

Still missing: Verified pedestrian minutes from exact hotel entrance/room tower to poker registration. No cross-resort walking time has been invented.

9. Which hotel is objectively highest quality for the lowest all-in price?

Known: Official hotel pages describe rooms and amenities; that does not provide a comparable independent quality measure.

Still missing: A quality standard, independent rating methodology and exact-date total rates. No unsupported hotel ranking is made.

10. What else affects eligibility or actual cost?

Known: Venetian structure sheets state players must be 21+ and have a Venetian Rewards card. Wynn WPT general terms state 21+ and require Wynn Rewards membership; event-specific terms apply.

Still missing: Player age/member status, registration deadlines, payment accepted, taxes/withholding, travel dates, budget, preferred poker formats, and tolerance for multi-day commitments.
