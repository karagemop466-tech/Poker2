Suggested Site & Decision-Making Improvements

Review each suggestion, pick the best path, then implement. Estimated effort and dependencies are given where known.

How to use this page. Read each suggestion, weigh impact vs. effort, then decide which to implement. Suggestions are not prioritized by us — you choose the order. Related open questions are cross-linked to Open Questions and Flagged for Review.

S-1 — Live hotel rate lookup with true-cost calculator

Impact: high • Effort: high • Depends on: Q6 (live rates)

Replace the blank rate cells with a real calculator: user picks dates and hotel, the site opens the hotel’s official booking page (direct link) and shows room rate + resort fee + 13.38% Clark County tax + parking = true cost per night, plus stay total. Because official booking engines are JavaScript-rendered and could not be scraped, the site should link out to official booking pages with the user’s dates pre-filled where the booking engine supports URL date parameters, rather than displaying scraped rates (which would go stale immediately).

Review before implementing: confirm which official booking engines support date-prefilled URLs; confirm tax treatment of resort fees (Q10); add a “not a quote” disclaimer.

S-2 — Re-entry / multi-entry policy extractor

Impact: high • Effort: medium • Depends on: Q1, Q11

Automatically parse each tournament’s official structure-sheet PDF for re-entry, rebuy, add-on, and multiple-flight language, and tag every event “single-entry”, “re-entry allowed”, “rebuys/add-ons”, or “multiple flights” with a link to the source. This directly supports your “single entry only” requirement and prevents misclassification (currently every Venetian entry policy is flagged unverified).

Review before implementing: decide whether to parse PDFs server-side or maintain a hand-verified policy list; link every tag to the official structure sheet.

S-3 — Verified walking distances and routes

Impact: high • Effort: medium • Depends on: Q8

Replace estimated walk times with Google Maps (or equivalent) walking-route distances and times between each hotel and each tournament venue, saved per route and re-checked regularly. Show “verified on ” and flag routes over 30 minutes.

Review before implementing: pick a mapping provider with a terms-of-use-compliant API; decide how often routes are re-verified (Strip construction changes walking routes).

S-4 — Calendar view and iCal export

Impact: medium • Effort: medium

A monthly calendar of single-entry tournaments (and a separate one for advantage events) with Add-to-Calendar (iCal) per event, time zone labeled Pacific, and a clash detector warning when two selected events overlap.

Review before implementing: source of truth is the official listings; builds on S-2 so only verified-single-entry events appear in the single-entry calendar.

S-5 — Budget and bankroll planner

Impact: medium • Effort: medium • Depends on: S-1

User enters trip dates, hotel, and target tournaments; the site computes total estimated cost (stay true cost + sum of buy-ins + estimated travel/food) and a “tournaments you can afford” list ranked by guarantee-per-dollar.

Review before implementing: label all outputs as estimates, not projections of winnings; no bankroll-management advice beyond arithmetic.

S-6 — Filtering and dashboard controls

Impact: medium • Effort: low

Filters on the tournament tables: by buy-in range, guarantee, date, game (NLH/PLO/Seniors/Ladies), entry type (verified single-entry vs. advantage), and venue. Toggle “show flagged/unverified rows”.

Review before implementing: keep the unfiltered official table as the source of truth; filters only hide rows, never alter data.

S-7 — Accessibility and mobile usability

Impact: medium • Effort: low–medium

WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, scalable text, touch-friendly tables (horizontal scroll on small screens), and a print-friendly stylesheet so a traveler can print the schedule.

Review before implementing: test with a screen reader and on a phone before release.

S-8 — Change monitoring for official pages

Impact: medium • Effort: medium

Periodically re-fetch the official Venetian, Wynn, Aria, Bellagio, and Caesars poker pages and highlight any change in dates, buy-ins, or guarantees, since poker schedules change without notice.

Review before implementing: respect site terms of use and robots.txt; use official pages only; log what changed and when.

S-9 — Saved itineraries and user accounts (optional)

Impact: low • Effort: high

Let users save a shortlist of tournaments and hotels, with estimated total cost, and email it to themselves. Only if the site grows beyond a personal planning tool.

Review before implementing: privacy and data-retention policy required; not needed for the first version.

S-10 — Illustrative expected-value estimates (analytical only)

Impact: low • Effort: low

Add a clearly-labeled illustrative column: guarantee ÷ buy-in ratio (already shown in the executive summary) with a warning that actual prize pools depend on field size and this is not a prediction of winnings. Do not add win-probability or ROI models unless clearly labeled as illustrative.

Review before implementing: keep it purely arithmetic; avoid anything that looks like gambling advice.

Decision rule. Before implementing any suggestion: (1) check the related open question on the Open Questions page, (2) confirm the data source is official, (3) decide priority and effort, (4) record the decision in Flagged for Review if it changes any displayed value.
