How to get a true cost per night. 1) Pick your dates (e.g. a tournament weekend such as Fri Nov 27 – Sun Nov 29, 2026, when WPT World Championship opens at Wynn and Venetian runs Events 14–16). 2) Open the hotel’s official booking link (column “Official sources”). 3) Note the room rate, add the mandatory resort fee, add Clark County room tax (13.38% per Venetian official terms; confirm whether tax also applies to the resort fee at check-in), plus parking if you drive. That total is the true cost per night. Room rates change daily — nothing below is a quote.
