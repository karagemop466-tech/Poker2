Do not use the archived numbers

The archived sites contain buy-ins, guarantees and resort fees that were typed in by hand and never traced to a source. Some are right. At least one is not: an early version listed a Horseshoe buy-in and a Caesars Palace resort fee that no official page states, and described MGM Grand's $120 daily as confirmed single-entry on the strength of silence. They are preserved here as a record, not as a reference.

Use the current dashboard instead. Every number on it links to the page it came from.

Why archive rather than delete

Three reasons, in order of importance. One: the brief asks for irregularities to be flagged and published, and a deleted dashboard cannot be inspected. Two: a later reader may need to know which number changed and when — the archive plus the new dataset is that diff. Three: deleting files rewrites nothing about the past; it only removes the evidence that the correction happened.

If the intent is to remove them from the published site entirely, delete the four trees and this page, and re-run the build. Nothing in the current dashboard links to them except this index and the navigation's own archive link.

Verification rule for this site. Every table row links to the official page, PDF or structure sheet the value came from, and shows the date that source was last read (2026-11-16). A cell showing — means no official source stated the value. Nothing is inferred or filled in. Guarantee ÷ entry fee is a descriptive ratio, not a forecast of winnings.
