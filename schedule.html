A realistic poker day plan

The ambition is to enter as many live tournaments as possible, but the source material gives scheduled start times—not how long each event will take or whether a bust-out permits another entry. This is therefore a start-time plan with explicit limits, not a promise of back-to-back play.

No 8:00 AM start was found in the official sources checked. Caesars' official guide describes a recurring Horseshoe NLH tournament at 9:00 AM, but does not give its dated lineup, buy-in, guarantee or entry-limit terms. A specifically dated Venetian satellite starts 9:10 AM on Dec 12 (not a direct event entry). The earliest dated direct Venetian starts reviewed are 10:10 AM.

Practical schedule recommendation

Conservative plan: Pick one primary tournament/day and one venue for the whole trip. Prioritize 10:10–11:10 AM direct starts if the goal is one tournament per day; use later starts as optional only after busting out and confirming registration. If “play all day” means continuous entry from 8 AM onward, the verified published schedules do not support that requirement. Do not book daily room moves based on overlapping start times.
Why not pack the table with a projected sequence? Tournament end times, ability to re-enter an event or register into another event while still in play, waitlists, capacity and live seat availability are absent from the sourced schedules. Any precise itinerary would invent constraints. See Flagged for review for venue questions that unlock a reliable schedule.
