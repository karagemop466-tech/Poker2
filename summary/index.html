Venetian house fee

Official structure sheets state the house fee for each buy-in (for example $58 of $300, $90 of $600, $100 of $800, $130 of $1,100). The prize pool share is therefore about 80–88% of the buy-in for the verified single-entry events.

Sources: [1] [2] [3] [4]

Wynn daily events are not single-entry

The official daily structure sheets allow re-entry on the Mon–Thu, Sat–Sun and nightly events, and rebuys and an add-on on the Friday event. These are excluded, even though the official poster does not mention re-entry.

Sources: [1] [2]

Wynn WPT events are unverified

Only the general terms of the WPT structures PDF have been read. The remaining sections are not reviewed, so the WPT rows are either UNVERIFIED or excluded by event name (multi-flight or satellite).

No 8:00 AM start can be confirmed

The earliest verified single-entry start at The Venetian is 11:10 AM. The earliest start on the schedule is 10:10 AM, and that event is excluded (multi-flight). A play-all-day plan from 8:00 AM cannot be built from verified single-entry events at these two properties.

Days with two or more verified single-entry events

Nine of the 23 days with Venetian rows have two or more verified single-entry events (typically one 11:10 AM event and one 5:10 PM event). A full single-entry day is therefore possible on those nine days, but the gap between events is not verified because event lengths are not stated.

Hotel resort fees are verified; room rates are not

Both resorts charge a resort fee of $55 plus tax per night. Applying the 13.38% county room tax from The Venetian's terms gives about $62.36, a computed figure with an assumption to confirm. No official nightly rate was captured, so no true nightly cost is shown.
