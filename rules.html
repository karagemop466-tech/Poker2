Structured entry-rule fields

Single-entry, re-entry, add-on, fee, guarantee, payout and bounty are stored as separate fields per event, each carrying its own status, the source's own words, and the date that source was retrieved. The Venetian fields come from that event's official structure sheet; the Wynn WPT fields from the official WPT terms document. “read · silent” is not a freezeout rule: it means the document was read in full and prints no such provision, which is why no row here is labelled confirmed single-entry.

27 Venetian rows carry an entry rule the sheet states in words (the multi-flight “may not play a subsequent flight” clause); 2 carry a stated further-buy-in price and are therefore spend-more by definition; 54 are read-and-silent candidates; and 166 rows across all venues have no rule document published anywhere.

Legend

  • stated — the document says it, and the quoted text is shown in the cell.
  • read · silent — the document was read in full and prints no such provision. Not a prohibition.
  • no document — no rule document exists for this row at all; the field is blank, never guessed.
  • stated on a single-entry field — the sheet states a rule that removes the event from a single-entry plan (a further buy-in is priced, or a flight restriction applies).
