The entry amount published by the property. It already includes the house fee — the Fee column breaks out how much of it the room withholds.
Fee
House fee withheld from the buy-in, as printed on the structure sheet ("$90 of the $600 Buy-In is withheld for house fees"). Blank where the sheet was not read.
Guarantee
The advertised guaranteed prize pool. For bounty events the property states the guarantee includes bounties paid, which is why bounty events are separated on the ranking page.
Chips / Level
Starting stack and minutes per level, from the property's printed schedule or structure sheet.
Late reg closes
The last moment a new entry is accepted. This is the column that decides whether a second tournament in the same day is even possible.
Entry rule
silent on entry limit = the event's own structure sheet was opened and says nothing about a maximum number of entries. sheet not read = nobody opened that event's sheet, so it is unverified rather than silent — a different and weaker state. re-entry / rebuy + add-on = the property's own terms positively permit paying again for more chips.
Type
Starting flights, day-2 restarts and final tables are labelled so they are not mistaken for additional tournaments.
Last checked
The date the linked source was read. Re-open the link before trusting the row.

Verification rule for this site. Every table row links to the official page, PDF or structure sheet the value came from, and shows the date that source was last read (2026-11-16). A cell showing — means no official source stated the value. Nothing is inferred or filled in. Guarantee ÷ entry fee is a descriptive ratio, not a forecast of winnings.
