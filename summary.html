Executive summary

Updated 2026-11-17 after this session’s structure-sheet verification pass. Scope, numbers and caveats below.

Scope

Covered: The Venetian (DeepStack Extravaganza IV, official schedule rows Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026) and Wynn Las Vegas (daily tournaments and WPT World Championship, Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026). Not covered: other Strip rooms. Every value is from an official source or is marked UNVERIFIED.

The current verified picture: 45 one-day events whose structure sheet is silent on re-entry (The Venetian DSE IV) — none of them has a published no-re-entry rule, 0 entry-ambiguous events, 37 spend-more events across Wynn, Caesars Palace, MGM Grand and Golden Nugget, plus recurring daily slates at Caesars Palace, Horseshoe and MGM Grand whose buy-ins are not all published.

What this means for an “8 AM to close” day: the earliest verified start is 9:00 AM. A day that begins at 8:00 AM cannot begin with a tournament; it begins with registration, breakfast, or a daily satellite. The trip planner therefore builds each day around the primary event and adds a conditional evening fallback instead of an impossible sequence.

What matters for a decision

  • Best verified value patterns: Venetian one-day events (sheet silent on re-entry) with a published guarantee — e.g. $300 for $10K GTD (33×) and $400 for $15K GTD (37.5×), house fee $58/$65, pool $242/$335 per entry. See ranking.
  • Do not mix in spend-more events without budgeting re-entries/add-ons: Wynn WPT re-entry events, Wynn Friday $240 rebuy, Caesars Palace $50 add-on, Golden Nugget re-entries/add-ons.
  • Hotel cost is dominated by resort fees: $55/night + tax at Venetian/Palazzo/Wynn ($62.36 with the 13.38% county tax applied), $50+ at MGM. True cost/night needs a dated quote — none captured yet.
  • Walking times are routed, not published. No casino prints one, so the figures come from Google Maps (read 2026-11-17) and are confirmed by an independent OSRM foot matrix: Wynn 12 min, Palazzo 4 min, Venetian 5 min, Caesars Palace 19 min, Horseshoe 24 min and Bellagio 28 min all clear the 30-minute limit to The Venetian Poker Room, while MGM Grand (42 min) and ARIA (37 min) do not. Wynn-side routes are unread.
  • Re-confirm everything before nonrefundable payment — schedules and fees change; the source monitor tracks official pages daily.

Venetian house fee

Official structure sheets state the house fee for each buy-in (for example $58 of $300, $90 of $600, $100 of $800, $130 of $1,100). The prize pool share is therefore about 80–88% of the buy-in for the verified single-entry events.

Wynn daily events are not single-entry

The official daily structure sheets allow re-entry on the Mon–Thu, Sat–Sun and nightly events, and rebuys and an add-on on the Friday event. These are excluded, even though the official poster does not mention re-entry.
