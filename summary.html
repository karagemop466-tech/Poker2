1. The bottom line

A fully optimised, bookable itinerary cannot be produced from official sources today. Not because the schedules are unknown — they are known in detail — but because three pieces a booking decision needs are published by nobody: a confirmed one-entry rule, a dated hotel total, and a walking time.

What can be done, and is done on this site, is narrower and still useful: every official start time, buy-in, fee split, guarantee, starting stack, level length and late-registration close for the Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV festival (Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026) is transcribed and linked, plus the rules of the competing Wynn, Bellagio, Caesars Palace and MGM Grand events.

2. What is genuinely verified

  • 83 Venetian event rows for the festival, from the official schedule PDF and all 37 official structure sheets.
  • Fee split per buy-in — e.g. $58 of every $300, $90 of every $600, $130 of every $1,100, $225 of every $2,200 — straight from the sheets.
  • Registration close times per event, printed on both the schedule PDF and each structure sheet, and they agree.
  • Guarantees per event, including the cases where the guarantee includes bounties.
  • Entry rules for four competing venues, quoted, including the permissive ones.
  • Venetian hotel economics: $55 resort fee plus tax, 13.38% Clark County room tax, $20/day guest parking, $150/night incidental hold, 3 PM check-in / 11 AM check-out.
  • Poker suite rate: from $119 (Venetian) / $209 (Palazzo), conditional on six hours of live cash play or a $300+ tournament entry per night.
  • No confirmed single-entry event, anywhere.
  • No dated room rate or checkout total.
  • No walking time between any hotel and any poker room.
  • No published expected finish time for any event.
  • No 08:00 AM start anywhere.
  • No room-tax percentage for Wynn, Bellagio, MGM Grand or the Caesars properties.
  • No expected field size, so no basis for an expected return.

5. Hotel position

On the published components, the Venetian/Palazzo is the only property where the poker room is in the same building complex as the rooms, and it is the only property that publishes both a room-tax rate (13.38%) and a poker-specific rate (from $119/$209, conditional). Wynn, Bellagio and MGM Grand each publish a $55 or $50 resort fee plus tax but no percentage; Caesars Palace and Horseshoe publish no resort-fee figure we could retrieve.

That is a component comparison across five properties, and a component comparison alone cannot rank them. A ranked hotel list needs a dated quote for the identical nights, which does not exist in this snapshot — see Hotels & true cost and F-04.

6. The honest recommendation

Objective that the evidence supports: maximise verified entries per day inside the Venetian festival window, using one-day events, and treat any second event on a day as a conditional fallback.

  1. Choose the week. The festival runs Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026; the Wynn WPT series overlaps Nov 27 – Dec 21, which is also when Wynn suspends its daily events.
  2. Get the one-entry answer in writing from the Venetian poker room (702.414.7657) for the one-day events you care about. Until then, budget as if a second entry is possible.
  3. Price one dated week at the Venetian with the poker rate (866.255.8936, code EPOKR25) and one at the public rate, then compare like-for-like in the calculator.
  4. Sequence from the day plan: one primary event per day, evening event only as a conditional fallback.
  5. Re-check the official pages, the policies and the rates immediately before any non-refundable payment.
